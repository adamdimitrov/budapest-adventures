8 Best Bike, E-Bike, Segway and E-Scooter Tours in Budapest (2026, Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
8 bike, e-bike, Segway and e-scooter tours compared on hills, hours, price per hour and rider limits
Last updated: Nov 18, 2026

Book the 3.5-hour city centre e-bike tour (€54) if you want one ride that covers both banks. It strings 19 stops together, from Heroes' Square and Andrássy Avenue to Fisherman's Bastion, Gellért Baths and the Central Market Hall, and the motor does the Castle Hill climb for you.

The hill decides everything else. Castle Hill is the one real climb on a Budapest wheeled tour: the classic-bike Grand Sightseeing ride (€38, 3 hours) still gets you up there and onto Margaret Island if you do not mind a hard pedal up the hill. Flat Pest-only bike loops start at €33. Segways (€45–69) and MonsteRoller e-scooters (from €34) take the hill with zero effort but come with rules: 14 or 15+ to drive a scooter, 30 to 140 kg on a Segway.

Below are 8 wheeled tours side by side on hours, price per hour, private vs group, which hills they actually climb and who is allowed to ride. Filter by bike, e-bike, Segway, e-scooter or private.

🏆 #1 Best Overall
Bike & Relax • ⏱️ 3.5 hours • 📍 Madách Imre út 12

Budapest: Guided City Centre Electric Bike Tour

4.9(731)
From €54
▾

Pros:

  • Castle Hill, Gellért Baths and the Pest boulevards in one loop
  • Electric assist takes the sting out of the Castle Hill climb
  • Shared groups capped at 10, private option on the same route
  • Guides in English, German and French
  • Highest guest score of the bike tours (4.9)

Cons:

  • Strict body limits: 160 cm minimum height, 110 kg maximum
  • No landmark entries, everything is seen from outside
  • Open chains can touch loose trousers
  • €16 more than the classic-bike Castle Hill tour at #2
📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

This is the widest loop on the page. In 3.5 hours you ride the full length of Andrássy Avenue to Heroes' Square and City Park, cut back through the Jewish Quarter to St Stephen's Basilica, Liberty Square and Parliament, cross the Chain Bridge and climb to Matthias Church and Fisherman's Bastion, then roll down past Rudas and Gellért Baths, over Liberty Bridge to the Central Market Hall and finish at the Dohány Street Synagogue. No other tour here strings both banks, both thermal baths on the Buda riverside and the Great Market together.

At €54, or €15.43 an hour, it costs more than the classic bike rides, and the difference is the motor. Castle Hill is the one real climb on any Budapest wheeled tour, and the operator puts it plainly: this is where the electric support shows its strength. You still have to pedal, so it is not a scooter, but the motor carries the climb.

The catch is the fine print. The operator excludes riders under 160 cm, over 110 kg, under 13 and anyone without cycling experience, and it uses bikes with open chains, so wear trousers that will not flap. If any of that rules you out, the Segway tours keep the Castle Hill view with a 30 to 140 kg window instead.

Castle Hill and Gellért Hill: Which Tours Climb Them

Budapest's Pest side is flat. The Buda side is not, and that is where the tours split:

  • Castle Hill (Fisherman's Bastion, Matthias Church): climbed on #1 e-bike, #2 classic bike, #4 Segway and #5 monster roller, and optional on #3 MonsteRoller. On a regular bike it is the one tough stretch.
  • Gellért Hill (Citadella): none of the bike or Segway tours climb it. #1 rides past Rudas and Gellért Baths at its foot. The only tour here with the Citadella on its stop list is the MonsteRoller (optional, by route).
  • Margaret Island: a 20-minute break on #2, and an option on the MonsteRoller routes.
  • Flat and Pest only: #6 Segway and #8 bike. #7 crosses to the Buda riverside at Castle Garden Bazaar but only passes Castle Hill.

If you want to stand inside Matthias Church or on the upper towers of Fisherman's Bastion rather than ride past, a walking tour with entries does that better: see our Buda Castle tours compared.

Before You Ride: Limits, What to Bring and What Costs Extra

  • All meeting points are on the Pest side, in Districts V and VII: Galamb utca 3 (#4, #5 and #6), Bécsi utca 8 (MonsteRoller), Semmelweis utca 14, Szent István tér 4 by the Basilica, Rumbach Sebestyén utca 10 and Madách Imre út 12. All are walkable from central hotels.
  • Bring ID for the scooter tours and the 90-minute Segway: #3, #5 and #6 ask for a passport or ID card (#5 and #6 accept a copy).
  • Shoes and clothes: flat, closed shoes. High heels are banned on the MonsteRoller and skirts are not advised; #1 warns that open chains can touch loose trousers.
  • Weather: #2, #3 and #7 state that they start in all weather. Ponchos are supplied on the Segway and scooter tours, gloves in winter, and the Segway Highlights tour (#4) can be rescheduled in heavy rain (over 1 mm per hour).
  • What costs extra: tips on every tour, the e-bike upgrade on #7, and food and drinks unless listed. Landmark entries are never included: you see Parliament, the Basilica and Matthias Church from outside.
  • Pair it with a soak: most bike routes pass Széchenyi Thermal Bath, so it is easy to come back later: compare tickets in our Budapest thermal baths guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Castle Hill hard on a regular bike?

It is the one real climb on any Budapest bike tour. The 3-hour Grand Sightseeing ride (#2) climbs it on a classic bike. If you want the view without the effort, pick the e-bike tour (#1), a Segway (#4) or a scooter (#3, #5).

E-bike, Segway or e-scooter: which is easiest?

A Segway or MonsteRoller needs no pedalling, and both start with a training session. An e-bike still needs normal cycling skills but helps on climbs. If you cannot ride a bike confidently in traffic, choose a Segway.

Do I need a driving licence for an e-scooter tour in Budapest?

No. The MonsteRoller tours need no licence, but drivers must be 14+ and bike or scooter experience is recommended. Top speed is 25 km/h.

Can children join a Budapest bike or Segway tour?

Yes, with limits. Kids from 8 can join the City Discovery bike tour (#8) and the 90-minute Segway (#6, minimum 30 kg). The Monster Roller tour (#5) lets under-15s ride behind an adult, and the MonsteRoller (#3) has back seats for ages 10 to 18 on request. Most other bike tours start at 12 or 13.

Are there weight or height limits?

Yes. The e-bike tour at #1 excludes riders under 160 cm or over 110 kg. Segways take 30 to 140 kg (#4), the Monster Roller tops out at 136 kg, and the 90-minute Segway needs at least 30 kg.

Which Budapest bike tour is cheapest?

The City Discovery Bike Tour (#8) has the lowest ticket at €33 for 2.5 hours. Per hour, the 3.5-hour bike tour with a certified guide (#7) is cheapest at €10.29.

Do the tours run in the rain?

Mostly yes. The Grand Sightseeing ride (#2), the MonsteRoller (#3) and the certified-guide bike tour (#7) state that they start in all weather, the Segway and scooter tours supply rain ponchos, and GetSegway's Segway Highlights tour (#4) can be rescheduled if rain is heavier than 1 mm per hour.
