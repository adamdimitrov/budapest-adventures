  • Summary: The 1-Hour Stag / Hen Private Party Boat experience is a standout option for those who want to celebrate their bachelor or bachelorette party in style on the Danube River.
  • Operating hours: The cruise operates from 8:00am and ending at 2:00am in the evening.
  • Amenities: Party on a cruise with sound system, special lights, and unlimited beer & wine for an hour. One shot included, but you can also buy other drinks onboard.
  • Price: At 400€, the cruise might seem a bit steep. However, considering the unlimited drinks, the private setting, and the capacity to accommodate up to 20 people, the cruise offers good value.

How to Get There

  • 🗺️ Meeting point: Budapest, Dock 42 port, Szent István park, 1138 Hungary
  • ❌ The meeting point is located in an inconvenient area near Margaret Island, making it inconvenient to reach.
  • 📘 How to get there: Use the 2 or 4/6 tram line to arrive at Jászai Mari tér. Then walk to Dock 42, which is located about 800m north from Margaret Bridge. It's quite the stroll.
  • As you approach dock 42, you'll see other docks with numbers from 10 to 0 first. Dock 42 comes after dock 0 (don't ask how they came up with this order). The vessel's name will be Neptun, but occasionally other boats may do the service as well.
