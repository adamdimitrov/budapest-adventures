🕵️ Local Guide Insider Secrets

Before you book, make sure you keep these verified travel guide tips in mind to maximize your cruise experience.

01

Timing is Everything

Always check the local sunset time. The perfect cruise leaves 30 minutes before sunset. You get stunning golden-hour photographs of Buda Castle, and by the time you turn back, the Hungarian Parliament will be illuminated in its full gold glory.

02

Arrive Early for Upper Deck Seating

Almost all operators use a first-come, first-served seating policy. If you want a prime spot on the open-air upper deck for photography, arrive at the dock at least 25 minutes before departure.

03

Locate Your Dock in Advance

Dock 11 (central, near Elizabeth Bridge) and Dock 42 (further north, past Margaret Bridge) are a 15-to-20 minute walk apart. Double-check your ticket so you don't end up sprinting to the wrong pier as the boat departs!
