The details a brochure leaves out

  • On some shared boats, drinks-only guests sit downstairs while dinner guests get the upper deck. Worth knowing before you book 40 seats.
  • For photos, pick a boat with a large open-air top deck and 360° views, like Legenda, Rubin Group or Portum.
  • Most dinner cruises I tested offer vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and lactose-free menus, but only on advance request.
  • Folklore shows often include a dance segment where guests join in. Great for a team night, less so for a quiet business dinner.
FAQ

Group cruise questions

How much does a group cruise in Budapest cost?

It depends on the boat, the format and the date. A seat on a sightseeing cruise costs far less than a dinner cruise or a private charter. Tell me your budget per person and I will only send options that fit it, with the operator's actual group price.

Do you charge a fee or add a markup?

No. The quote is free and you pay the cruise company directly at their price. Some operators pay me a referral commission, which never changes what you pay.

How quickly will I get a quote?

The same day you send the form. If I need operator confirmation for a busy date, I tell you that straight away and follow up as soon as I have it.

Can the whole group sit together?

That is one of the first things I check. On a dinner cruise the operator reserves tables for your group; for full privacy, a private boat is the only option where nobody else is on board.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes. Most dinner cruises I have tested offer vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and lactose-free options when they are requested in advance. Add the numbers in the form and I pass them on.

What about a small group, like 8 or 12 people?

For small groups a private boat is often the easiest choice, and some take up to 12 guests. Compare them in my private boat tour guide, or send the form and I will suggest one.

Do you work with tour operators and travel agencies?

Yes. If you bring groups to Budapest regularly, choose "Tour operator / travel agency" as the occasion and tell me roughly how many groups and dates you have.

Ready to plan your group cruise?

Send your date and headcount. You will have a shortlist and a quote today.
