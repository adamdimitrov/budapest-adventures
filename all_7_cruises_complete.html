About Silverline Cruises

Silverline is an established company operating in Budapest since at least 2011. They manage a solid range of cruise offerings and run some of the most popular cruises in both the sightseeing and dinner categories on the Danube.

Arriving to Location

The Silverline dock is located in the city center (Dock 11), right next to the Elisabeth Bridge. After a quick ticket check, we boarded the boat, walking across a small gangway to get on. The ship departed promptly on schedule.

The Ship

The dinner takes place on the upper deck where you're seated at 2 or 4-person tables. The vessel is a modern twin-hull catamaran with exceptional stability on the river.

Dinner Venue / Seating / Vibe

The ship's lower deck hosts the larger folklore show dinner.

The ship's first deck features a large-capacity area with a small central stage. On the upper deck, where our no-show dinner was seated, the vibe is noticeably calmer, cozier, and more conversational.

The tables on the upper deck are arranged neatly along the panoramic windows. The sliding frames let you breathe in the cool evening river breeze and snap crystal-clear photographs with zero window reflection.

Dinner Cruise Menu

The dinner menu allows you to choose from one appetizer, two types of soup, three main courses, and three desserts. Please note that certain dishes may change based on seasonal availability.

Course 1: Appetizer

Caponata Schiacciata: Homemade Italian-style toast with a creamy eggplant topping. It was served immediately upon sitting down, which was warmly appreciated while our welcome drinks were poured.

Course 2: Soups

Chicken Dumpling Soup with Tarragon: The tarragon flavor was distinctly noticeable and fragrant. It was one of the best soups we've had in a long time, featuring fresh, crisp vegetables that were not overcooked.

Traditional Hungarian Goulash Soup: Rich, hearty, and authentic, packed with tender beef cuts, root vegetables, and traditional sweet Hungarian paprika broth.

Course 3: Main Courses

Pesto Chicken Roulade with Risotto Bianco: The chicken was divinely tender with a pesto filling and spicy Parmesan. The risotto was slightly runnier than ideal but still very good. Overall flavor profile was an impressive 10 out of 10.

Beef Stew with Hungarian Dumplings (Nokedli): Deeply savory, slow-braised beef melting into a thick paprika onion gravy, served with authentic homemade noodles.

Roasted Trout Fillet: Pan-seared with a crisp skin, served with fresh lemon and buttery parsley potatoes — a lighter option executed cleanly.

Course 4: Desserts

Pistachio Tiramisu (Extra charge of +2,460 HUF): Creamy and pleasant, though in our honest editorial opinion, it is not worth paying the additional fee when the included desserts are already great.

Avocado Chocolate Mousse: Tasted primarily like dark chocolate with a smooth texture; a solid vegan option.

Somlói Galuska (Hungarian Trifle): Sponge cake layers soaked in chocolate sauce, walnuts, and topped with fresh whipped cream.

During the two-hour boat trip, we admired all of Budapest's main sights, sailing between Margaret Island and the National Athletics Center. The boat executes a full double loop, ensuring passengers on both sides of the salon get unobstructed front-row views of the illuminated Hungarian Parliament, Buda Castle, and Fisherman's Bastion.
