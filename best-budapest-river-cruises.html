By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest River Cruise Specialist
6 of 12 Cruises Tested in Person • 100+ Original Photos & Videos
Last updated: Nov 12, 2026

Dozens of boats sail the same loop past the Parliament, and from the pier they all look alike. What you are really paying for is the ticket: one welcome drink or 75 minutes of unlimited prosecco, a 30-language audio guide or none at all, a guaranteed rooftop seat or a candlelit dinner. Tickets run from €11 to €110 for much the same views.

So I rode the most popular cruise of every major company in Budapest myself, added a few well-reviewed newcomers, and compared the 12 worth your evening side by side below, with my own photos and videos. Want to eat on board? See the best dinner cruises in Budapest.
