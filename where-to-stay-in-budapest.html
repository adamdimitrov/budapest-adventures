Where to Stay in Budapest: 6 Best Areas (2026, with 2 Hotels in Each)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
6 areas compared on walking distance, metro, atmosphere and hotel prices, with 2 well-rated hotels in each
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right area to stay in Budapest is the one that fits your trip: District V downtown if it is your first visit and you want to walk everywhere (start with Prestige Hotel by the Chain Bridge), District VI around the Opera and Andrássy Avenue for grand boulevards, District VII for ruin bars and the Jewish Quarter, or the Castle District for quiet cobbled lanes. Staying near a spa instead? See the spa hotels. This page gets you to the right area, and a hotel in it, in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared 6 areas on what decides where you sleep: how far you walk to the Basilica, Parliament and the river, the nearest metro, the atmosphere at night, and what a well-rated hotel costs there on the same night, with 2 hotels per area checked on each hotel's own listing. Getting Around From Each Area

Metro: District V and VI sit on the M1, M2 and M3 lines (Deák Ferenc tér, Opera, Arany János utca). District VII uses Astoria and Blaha Lujza tér (M2), District VIII Corvin-negyed and Rákóczi tér. The Castle District and Margaret Island have no metro; you get there by bus.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best area to stay in Budapest?

For a first visit, District V (Belváros and Lipótváros) on the Pest side: the Basilica, Parliament, the Chain Bridge and Váci utca are all walkable.

Is it better to stay in Buda or Pest?

Pest for most visitors: it has the main sights, restaurants and metro lines. Buda's Castle District suits couples who want quiet, historic streets and do not mind buses.

Where should I stay in Budapest for nightlife?

District VII, the Jewish Quarter, where the ruin bars are. Roombach is 1,200 ft from the Dohány Street Synagogue; Queen's Court has a spa to recover in.

What is the cheapest good area to stay in Budapest?

District VIII. Bo18 (9.2) costs €79 and Central Hotel 21 (8.8) €64 for two on a March weeknight, both within walking distance of the National Museum.

Where to stay in Budapest with kids?

District VI's suite hotels, like Mamaison Residence Izabella with full kitchens and a children's breakfast discount, or Margaret Island for green space and pools.
