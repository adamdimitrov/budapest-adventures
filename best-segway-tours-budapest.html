9 Best Segway and E-Scooter Tours in Budapest (2026, Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
9 Segway, e-scooter and EZRaider rides compared on route, ride time, price per hour and rider limits
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right Segway or e-scooter tour is the one that fits your day: a first 90-minute ride past Parliament with your own guide (that is the #1 pick, €45), a 2-hour loop that rolls up to Fisherman's Bastion, a wide-wheel e-scooter that covers more ground, or an evening ride when the riverside is quieter. If you would rather pedal, the bike and e-bike tours have their own comparison. This page gets you to that ride in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared all 9 on what really decides it: whether the route climbs Castle Hill, ride time and price per hour, private or shared group, age and weight limits, and what costs extra, checked on each seller's own page. Segway, MonsteRoller or EZRaider: Which Is Easiest?

Segway: the easiest to learn. Every Segway tour here starts with 10–15 minutes of training, and you lean to move. The catch is the weight window (most cap riders at 125–140 kg and need 29–30 kg minimum) and the need to step on and off unaided.

MonsteRoller wide-wheel e-scooter (#3, #9): faster (up to 25 km/h) and closer to riding a bike. No licence is needed, but the operator recommends bike or scooter experience, drivers must be 14+, and the guided tour gives no refund if you cannot ride after practice.

EZRaider (#8): four wheels and hand controls make it the most stable, and the only one that takes small children (in tandem from 3).

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Segway tour in Budapest cost?

From €35 for the 1-hour Express to €86 for the 3-hour Grand City Tour. The best value per hour is the 2-hour Buda and Pest ride at €54 (€26.80 an hour).

Do Segway tours in Budapest go up to Buda Castle?

Some do. The 2-hour Buda & Pest ride (#2), the Castle District tour (#4), the 3-hour Grand City tour (#6) and the night ride (#7) climb to Fisherman's Bastion and Matthias Church. The 90-minute (#1) and 1-hour (#5) rides stay in Pest.

Can kids do a Segway tour in Budapest?

From age 6 on #2, 7 on #6 and #7, 8 on #1, 9 on #5 and 10 on #4, usually with a 29–30 kg minimum weight. For younger children, the EZRaider (#8) takes kids from 3 in tandem with an adult.

Do I need a licence for an e-scooter in Budapest?

No. The wide-wheel MonsteRoller counts as an e-bike and uses cycle lanes. Drivers on the guided tour must be 14+, and bike or scooter experience is recommended.

Is there a weight limit on Segways?

Yes: 125 kg on the Castle District tour (#4), 129 kg on the Express (#5) and 140 kg on #2. The minimum is about 29–30 kg.

Segway or e-scooter: which should I pick?

A Segway is easier to learn and every tour starts with training. A MonsteRoller is faster and feels like a bike, so pick it if everyone in your group rides a bike confidently.
