🥗 Appetizers & Starters

Guests choose from 6 starters. We tasted both the Cold Goose Liver (Foie Gras) and the Tartar Beefsteak with garlic cream. The goose liver was exceptionally smooth, rich, and paired harmoniously with warm brioche, while the steak tartare was freshly hand-prepared with crisp red onion.

🥣 Soups Course

Two warm soup options are served. We ordered the Pumpkin Cream Soup with toasted pine nuts and basil oil (rating: 4.5/5). It arrived piping hot with rich seasonal velvety thickness, providing a cozy opening as the boat pulled away from Vigadó tér.

🥩 Main Courses — Duck vs. Scallop

Six mains are offered. The standout is the Rosemary Duck Breast with layered sweet potato, quince, and fig—wonderfully tender with a crisp skin. The seared Scallops with chili were cooked accurately without becoming rubbery. Other choices include Roasted Trout with lemongrass, Beef Confit with Dijon carrot purée, and Mushroom Risotto.

🍰 Desserts & Sweets

Dessert wraps up with 3 choices: Panna Cotta with forest fruit rose jam, Sweet Dream Chocolate Cake with strawberry ragout, or the Chocolate Brownie with coconut foam and salted caramel. Both the brownie and cheesecake provided clean, balanced sweetness.

🌱 Dietary Options (Veg & GF)

The kitchen provides thoughtful vegetarian, gluten-free, and dairy-free options marked directly on the menu. Note: There are currently no dedicated vegan main courses, so let Legenda know in advance if you have severe dietary restrictions.

🍷 Welcome Drink & Wine List

Every ticket includes one full welcome drink: choose between Hungarian sparkling champagne, local white/red wine, premium draft beer, or chilled mineral water/soft drink. An extensive list of renowned Tokaj, Villány, and Eger wines is available for purchase onboard.

✨ Custom All-Glass Vessels

Legenda operates two identical sister ships custom-engineered for Budapest dinner cruising. Unlike converted cargo barges or vintage paddle wheelers, these vessels feature continuous wrap-around curved glass panels and an electric retractable glass roof that preserve unimpeded sightlines from every angle.

🪑 100% Window-Angled Table Geometry

The boat's layout is its greatest engineering feat: a central walkway with a single row of outward-canted tables along both sides. This ensures that every couple sits directly facing the glass—nobody has their back turned to the illuminated city.

❄️ Climate Control & Smooth Cruising

Both ships feature modern heating and air-conditioning systems, keeping the indoor salon comfortably warm in winter and crisp during humid summer evenings. The hull moves smoothly through the water with minimal vibration or diesel noise.

🔭 Complete Danube UNESCO Route

The 2.5-hour route covers the full UNESCO World Heritage Danube banks: departing Dock 7 Vigadó tér, sailing south past the Palace of Arts and National Theatre, then turning north for an unhurried, breathtaking glide directly past Buda Castle, Fisherman's Bastion, the Chain Bridge, Parliament, and Margaret Island.

🎵 Live Lounge Music & Romantic Ambiance Entertainment ▾

🎹 Live Acoustic Salon Duo

Throughout the 2.5-hour sailing, a live pianist and female vocalist perform tasteful international jazz, acoustic pop classics, and romantic standards. The vocals were polished, subtle, and reminiscent of high-end Seine dinner cruises in Paris rather than loud tourist folklore shows.

💬 Conversation-Friendly Acoustics

Crucially, the musical performance is kept at a comfortable background volume. You can have an intimate conversation across your candlelit table without raising your voice, creating an atmosphere that feels sophisticated rather than theatrical.

📍 Dock 7 Vigadó tér — Pier, Boarding & How to Get There Location & Map ▾

Legenda City Cruises departs from Dock 7 at Vigadó tér on the Pest riverbank (Jane Haining rakpart, 1052 Budapest), situated halfway between the Chain Bridge and Elizabeth Bridge directly opposite the Marriott Hotel.

🚶 Walking from City Center

It takes only 5 minutes to walk down to Dock 7 from Vörösmarty tér (Metro line M1) or 8–10 minutes from Deák Ferenc tér (interchange for Metro lines M1, M2, and M3). Walk west toward the river promenade and take the steps down to the quay level.

🚋 Scenic Tram 2 Access

The famous scenic Danube Tram Line 2 and 2B stops directly at Vigadó tér. From the tram platform, cross the pedestrian walkway down to Jane Haining rakpart and look for the illuminated Dock 7 (Legenda) pontoon.

⏱️ Boarding & Arrival Tips

Boarding begins 15–20 minutes before sailing. Arrive at Dock 7 around 19:25 (in summer) or 18:40 (in winter). Present your smartphone QR ticket at the dock gate; waitstaff will escort you directly to your reserved candlelit table.
