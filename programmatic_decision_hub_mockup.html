Frequently Asked Questions: Budapest River Cruises

Crucial practical answers before booking your tickets on the Danube.

What happens if it rains during our scheduled cruise?

All major boats (Legenda, Silverline, Mahart) feature enclosed, heated panoramic lower salons. You will have full 360° visibility through floor-to-ceiling glass without getting wet. Cruises only cancel during extreme Danube water levels or violent hail storms, in which case full refunds or reschedules are guaranteed.

What time do the Budapest Parliament lights turn on and off?

The Hungarian Parliament illuminates precisely at dusk according to the city lighting schedule. Under current municipal energy measures, the decorative architectural lighting turns off at 23:00 (11:00 PM) sharp. We strongly advise booking departures between 19:30 and 21:30 to see the full illumination.

Can I purchase tickets at the pier dock or should I book in advance?

While Legenda has a physical box office at Pier 7, evening slots between 19:00 and 21:00 frequently sell out 1–3 days in advance during spring, summer, and autumn. Dinner cruises require catering preparation and cannot be booked upon arrival. Instant smartphone QR tickets can be booked online up to 30 minutes before departure.
