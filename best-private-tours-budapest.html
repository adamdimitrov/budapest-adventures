9 Best Private Tours and Private Guides in Budapest (2026, Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
9 private tours compared on price per person at your group size, hours, transport, pick-up and languages
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right private tour is the one that fits your trip: a guide and a driver who cover Buda and Pest door to door (that is the #1 pick, from €118), a local who walks you through the city your way for about €40, an open Jeep, tuk-tuk or rickshaw so nobody gets tired, or a focused walk through the Castle District or the Jewish Quarter. If you are happy to share with strangers, the group walking tours cost less. This page gets you to the right guide in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared all 9 on what decides a private booking: the price per person at your group size (some charge per head, some per group), hours, car or on foot, hotel pick-up, a live guide or an audio guide, and the languages on offer, checked on each seller's own page. Live Guide, Driver Commentary or Audio Guide?

"Private" means different things on these tours. A live guide walks or rides with you and answers questions: #1 (with a separate driver), #2, #4, #6, #8 and #9. On the tuk-tuk (#5) your guide is also the driver. On the Taycan (#7) the driver explains the sights as they drive. On the Jeep (#3) the driver speaks English and the commentary comes from an audio guide in 12 languages, which is why it is the cheapest per head for a group.

If the stories matter most, book a live guide. If the ride and the photos matter most, the Jeep and the Taycan are more fun.

  • Family or friends, 4 to 6: #3 Jeep or #6 Castle District walk.
  • Older travellers or limited walking: #5 tuk-tuk or #8 e-rickshaw.
  • A special occasion: #7 Porsche Taycan.

Before You Book: Pick-up, Tickets and Timing

Pick-up: eight of the nine offer pick-up or meet you at your accommodation; only the Jewish heritage walk (#9) starts at a fixed point (Kossuth Lajos utca 7–9).

Tickets are never included. Entries to Matthias Church, museums, synagogues and the Parliament are extra on every tour here. The Parliament needs a timed ticket bought ahead: see Budapest Parliament tickets.

Timing: #1 suggests starting between 8.30 and 10 am, #4 at 9 am or 2 pm; the tuk-tuk can be cancelled for bad weather with a new date or refund.

Accessibility: #1 and #2 are listed as wheelchair accessible; the Jeep and the Castle District walk are not.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a private tour guide cost in Budapest?

From €40 per person for a custom private walk (#2) to €169 per person for a full private city tour by vehicle (#4). Per-group tours get cheaper with more people: the Castle District walk is €120 for up to 6 (€20 each at 6).

Which private tour is best for a family?

For 4 to 6 people, the Russian Jeep or G-Wagon tour (#3, €210 per vehicle) or the Buda Castle private walk (#6, €120 per group). With small kids who tire quickly, pick the Jeep.

Do private tours include hotel pick-up?

Eight of the nine do or meet you at your accommodation. Only the Jewish heritage walk (#9) starts at a fixed meeting point.

Are entrance fees included?

No. Matthias Church, museums, synagogues and the Parliament are extra on every tour here. Some operators help you book them.

Can I get a private guide in my language?

Yes: #2 and #9 guide in English, Spanish, French and Italian, #4 in English, Italian and German, #1 in English, German and Hungarian, #8 in English, Spanish, Portuguese and Hungarian, and the Jeep (#3) has an audio guide in 12 languages.

Is a private tour worth it over a group tour?

For 4 or more people, often yes: the Castle District private walk costs €30 each at 4. For a couple, a group walking tour is cheaper; the private walk (#2) at €40 each is the closest in price.
