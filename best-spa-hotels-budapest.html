7 Best Spa and Thermal Hotels in Budapest (2026, Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
7 spa hotels compared on the pools and spa you get, price per night, guest scores and distance from the centre
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right spa hotel in Budapest is the one that fits your stay: thermal water in the building, free for guests (that is Ensana Thermal on Margaret Island, the #1 pick, €111), a spa suite in the centre so the sights are walkable, a rooftop pool for summer, or a big pool with river views. If you only want a day of bathing, the thermal baths are compared separately. This page gets you to the right hotel in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared all 7 on what decides a spa stay: which pools and saunas are listed (thermal or not, indoor or rooftop), the price for the same night, guest scores, and how far you are from downtown, checked on each hotel's own listing. 📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

Ensana Thermal is the only hotel here with thermal water in the building, free for guests: a thermal pool, a recreational pool, a 56 ft swimming pool, a seasonal outdoor pool, several saunas and a steam room. For the same night it cost €111, less than every central spa hotel except Continental.

It sits on the quiet northern end of Margaret Island, next to the Japanese Garden, and its medical centre runs treatment programmes for back, joint and stress problems at extra cost.

The catch is the location: 2.5 miles from downtown, so plan on buses or taxis for sightseeing. For a bathing holiday, it is the best value in Budapest.

Before You Book: Spa Rules, Seasons and Bath Days

Kids and pools: Corinthia lets guests under 16 use the pool and spa tub only from 9 am to 3 pm, with an adult.

Seasonal pools: the outdoor pools at Ensana Thermal and on Continental's rooftop are summer-only. The indoor pools run all year.

Treatments cost extra: the medical and spa treatments at the Ensana hotels and Helia are paid on top of the room.

Add a public bath: staying central? A day at Széchenyi or Rudas fits easily; on a Saturday, Sparty is the night-time version.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Budapest hotels have thermal water?

Ensana Thermal Margaret Island has thermal pools free for guests, and Ensana Grand Margaret Island next door connects to the same spa through a heated underground hall.

What is the best spa hotel in central Budapest?

Kozmo Hotel Suites & Spa has the highest guest score here (9.5), with an indoor pool, sauna and hot tub, 1 mile from downtown. Queen's Court is the cheaper central option with a pool and kitchenette suites.

Which hotel in Budapest has a rooftop pool?

Continental Hotel Budapest lists a rooftop pool, open in the summer months, plus an indoor pool.

How much is a spa hotel in Budapest per night?

From €97 (Continental) to €163 (Kozmo) for 2 adults on a March weeknight. The two thermal hotels on Margaret Island cost €104–111.

Is the spa free for hotel guests?

At Ensana Thermal and Ensana Grand the spa area is free for guests; treatments cost extra. Check the other hotels' rate details for spa access.

How far is Margaret Island from the city centre?

The Ensana hotels are 2.4–2.5 miles from downtown, on the quiet northern half of the island.
