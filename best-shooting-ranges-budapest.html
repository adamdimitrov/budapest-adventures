Best Shooting Ranges in Budapest in 2026 (9 Packages Compared)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
9 shooting packages compared on guns, rounds, price per shot, location and rules
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right shooting package is the one that fits your group: a first go with an AK-47 in the city centre, an outdoor range with hotel pickup, the most guns and rounds for your money, or a stag do with the whole group. This page gets you to that range in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared all 9 packages across four ranges on what really decides it: guns, rounds, price per shot, indoor vs outdoor, transfers and the rules (18+, ID, strictly sober), checked on each seller's own page. Shooting on a Stag or Hen Do in Budapest

A range session is a strong stag or hen afternoon, and it works best before the drinking starts. Every range here bans alcohol and intoxication outright, and Churchill states that all visitors must be sober. Turn up after a long lunch with beers and you can be turned away.

Group pricing: all packages are priced per shooter. A group of 8 on the Shooting2 shotgun package pays 8 × €73.65 = €589; on Capital it is 8 × €86.28 = €690. Mixed groups work well at Ranger, where each person can choose between one gun and up to 10 at the range.

Group size: Shooting2 caps each session at 6 participants, so a group of 10 needs two back-to-back slots or a different range. Capital and Churchill do not state a cap, and Capital explicitly caters to stag parties, birthdays and team events. Ranger's outdoor package includes the bus for the whole group, which removes the taxi logistics.

How to book: put everyone on one booking so you land in the same slot, make sure every shooter brings a passport or ID card (Capital, Shooting2 and Ranger require it; Ranger accepts a copy), and check that nobody in the group is under 18. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before applies to all nine packages, so you can lock in a slot early.

Make it a day: shoot in the afternoon, then hand over to the evening. Our pub crawl comparison covers Budapest's ruin bar routes, and the Széchenyi Sparty guide covers Budapest's Saturday-night bath party. More ideas for a group weekend are in the best things to do in Budapest.

Rules, Getting There and What Costs Extra

Who can shoot: 18+ only at every range, no licence or experience required. Every package that lists restrictions excludes pregnant women. Churchill adds limits: no people with heart problems, and a minimum weight of 40 kg on the Hollywood and Soviet packages (plus 140 cm minimum height on Soviet), with upper age limits of 75–80.

What to bring: a passport or ID card (the Capital, Shooting2 and Ranger packages say so explicitly), and your phone: photos are welcome at Capital and Shooting2 when your instructor says so. Ranger supplies eye and ear protection, a protective vest and targets.

Getting there: three of the ranges are walkable from central hotels. Shooting2 is on Podmaniczky Frigyes tér near St Stephen's Basilica and Arany János utca metro (entrance between the café and the burger restaurant); Capital is at Dob utca 89 and Churchill at Holló utca 6, both in District VII. Ranger Indoor is at Maglódi út 47 in District X. Only Ranger Outdoor includes a transfer, from central pick-up points about 20 minutes from the range.

Timing: Churchill sessions take about an hour, Capital and Shooting2 about 1.5 hours including registration and coffee (arrive 15 minutes early), and Ranger runs 2-hour lessons, or 2–3.5 hours with the outdoor transfer. Indoor ranges are weather-proof, which makes them a good rainy-day plan.

What costs extra: extra guns and extra rounds at every range. Capital and Shooting2 sell add-ons on site, and Ranger prices extra guns and ammunition on top of its €85 entry package. Decide your limit before you start.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a shooting range cost in Budapest?

Packages run from €73.65 to €225 per person. Most first-timer packages cost €81–90 for 40–60 rounds, which is about €1.50–2.20 per shot. The Soviet-gun package at €225 is the outlier.

Can I shoot an AK-47 in Budapest?

Yes. The Capital 6-gun package and all three Shooting2 packages include 5 rounds on an AK-47 for €74–90. Churchill's Soviet package gives 7 AK-47 rounds plus 7 on the short AKSU for €225. Churchill's Hollywood package includes 5 AK-47 rounds in one of its two advertised line-ups; the Basic package has none.

Do I need a licence or experience to shoot in Budapest?

No. Every package here is designed for beginners, with a safety briefing and an instructor or range master supervising. You must be 18 or over, and Capital, Shooting2 and Ranger ask every shooter to bring a passport or ID card.

Can I drink before going to a shooting range in Budapest?

No. All ranges ban alcohol and intoxication, and Churchill states that all visitors must be sober. On a stag do, shoot first and drink after.

Is there a shooting range in Budapest with hotel pickup?

Yes: Ranger's outdoor package includes an air-conditioned bus from central pick-up points to the range about 20 minutes away and back, from €85.

Which Budapest shooting range is best for a stag party?

For a big group, Capital (stag parties catered for, no stated cap) or Ranger Outdoor (bus included, everyone picks their own gun count). Shooting2 offers the best value but caps sessions at 6, so larger groups need two slots. Pair it with a pub crawl in the evening.

How long does a shooting experience take?
