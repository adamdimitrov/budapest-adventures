Every Budapest dinner cruise promises the illuminated Parliament, a gourmet dinner and live music, and they all have thousands of good reviews. Prices run from €44 for a goulash supper to €110 for a 4-course candlelit dinner. So my friends and I booked and rode 3 of them ourselves: Legenda, Silverline and Hungária Koncert.

We judged the food, what the ticket includes, the service, the music, the boat and how much of Budapest you actually see from your table. Below is the short version: which one won, why, and when one of the other two is the better pick for you. All 6 cruises I compare, including the 3 I have not personally tested, are in my complete dinner cruise ranking.

Why Legenda Took the #1 Spot

Silverline is the better deal and Hungária Koncert is the more Hungarian night out. But Legenda was the only one that felt like a restaurant dinner rather than a tourist evening: more dishes to choose from, attentive service, quiet live music and a glass boat with an open top deck. If you only do one dinner cruise in Budapest and it is for a special night, this is it. Here is what I found on board.

1. A Real 4-Course Dinner, Not a Banquet

You pick one dish per course on board, from 6 starters, 2 soups, 6 mains and 3 desserts. That is 17 dishes, the widest choice of the three cruises I tested. Silverline has 1 starter and 4 mains; Hungária Koncert has 3 fixed set menus.

The food photos here are from my test dinner. The menu rotates, so the dishes on your night will differ: the current menu is below.

2. Live Lounge Music You Can Talk Over

A pianist and singer duo plays international standards and romantic songs. There is no folk show and no loud stage act, so you can actually have a conversation. Tables for two sit along the windows, with larger tables for groups, and they are spaced well apart.

3. A Glass Boat With an Open Top Deck

The salon is enclosed in glass, with air conditioning and heating, so the views work from your table in any season. You can step up to the open-air top deck at any point for photos. The 2.5-hour route passes Parliament, Buda Castle, the Chain Bridge, Fisherman's Bastion and Gellért Hill.

4. Service That Felt Like a Restaurant

The English-speaking staff were professional and attentive, refilled drinks promptly and paced the courses well. White tablecloths, polished glassware and soft table lighting give it the "James Bond" feel I remember most from the evening.

Included in the ticket

  • 4-course dinner for adults, 3 courses for children.
  • A welcome drink: a glass of champagne, wine, beer, a soft drink or mineral water.
  • Live lounge music from the pianist and singer duo.
  • A candlelit table for two or more.

Not included: any other drinks. Each main has a recommended Hungarian wine, sold by the bottle from €15 (Dúzsi Rozé) to €39 (St. Andrea Áldás Bikavér).

What I Didn't Love

  • It is not cheap. From €110, it costs €33 more than Silverline's no-show dinner and €31 more than Hungária Koncert. For a special night I think it is worth it; for a casual evening it may not be.
  • Only one drink is included. Wine with dinner is extra, by the bottle, so budget for it.
  • No Hungarian folk show. The music is international lounge, not csárdás. If you want folk music and dancers, Hungária Koncert or Silverline's Folk & Operetta evening is the better pick.
  • The candles are not real. Maritime safety rules mean the table candles are flameless LED votives. It still looks lovely, but it is not a real flame.

Quick Questions

Which menu should I pick?

There is one menu and you choose your dishes on board, one per course. Vegetarians have a goat cheese mousse starter and a cauliflower steak main. For the full experience, add the recommended wine for your main: the bottles start at €15.

What is the dress code?

It is a white-tablecloth, candlelit dining room, so dress as you would for a nice restaurant dinner. You will be more comfortable with a layer if you plan to go up on the open top deck after dark.

Can I bring kids?

Yes. Children aged 3 to 12 pay €84 and get a 3-course menu. Strollers are fine. Wheelchair access is limited and electric wheelchairs are not allowed, so contact Legenda before you book.

Where exactly do I board?

Legenda City Cruises, Dock 7, Jane Haining rakpart, 1051 Budapest, on the riverbank between Elizabeth Bridge and Chain Bridge. Take tram 2 to Vigadó tér. Boarding starts at 18:30 for the 19:00 departure; arrive 20 to 30 minutes early.

My pick for your Budapest dinner cruise

Legenda's Candlelit Dinner Cruise, 19:00 from Dock 7. Tables for two line the windows, and the open top deck is there when you pass Parliament.
