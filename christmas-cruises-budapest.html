The 9 Best Christmas Cruises in Budapest (2026)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Local Budapest Tester
Budapest Local • Every Bookable Cruise Compared Side by Side
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right Christmas cruise is the one that fits your holiday: a four-course Christmas dinner on the Danube, a market boat with unlimited mulled wine, a folk show and open bar on Christmas Eve, or New Year's Eve on the river. This page gets you to that boat in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared all 9 cruises sailing over Christmas on what really decides it: what runs on which day (24, 25 or 26 December), the menu, what is really included and price, checked on each seller's own page. Filter by day, dinner, drinks or price below, or scroll straight to the ranking.

Summary & Review Overview ▾

Silverline’s seasonal 1-hour cruise with a mug of spiced pomegranate winter punch and a Cinnabon chimney cake: a kürtőskalács with brown sugar, cinnamon and a cream cheese and mascarpone frosting. It passes Parliament, Buda Castle and the lit bridges.

It’s an Advent cruise, not a Christmas Day one: the booking calendar runs until 23 December, with eight sailings a day from 11:00 to 21:00. That makes it an easy add-on to an afternoon at the Christmas markets.

It’s new this season, so there are no reviews yet. It’s also bookable online.
