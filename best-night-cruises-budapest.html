Sightseeing & Views

  • Like on any boat tour in Budapest, the cityscape is breathtaking throughout the trip. The best spot for sightseeing on this cruise is the top deck.
  • The cruise itinerary covers all of Budapest's landmarks, including the National Opera and the Palace of Arts.

Rooftop Waiting Area

  • By ascending the stairs, you can wait in the rooftop open-air waiting area, where you can enjoy the fresh air and scenic views of Budapest.

Welcome Drink

  • The cruise kicks off with receiving a complimentary welcome drink: choose from champagne, wine, beer, a soft drink, or mineral water. You also get to enjoy an extra free lemonade in the summer season.
💡 Tip: For the best sightseeing and photo opportunities, choose a ship with large open-air decks that offer unobstructed 360° views, like Legenda's.
During the Cruise: Ship, Lower vs. Top Deck & Views Onboard Walkthrough ▾

The Ship(s)

  • Legenda City Cruises operates three sightseeing vessels, all of which offer a similar experience but differ primarily in size:
    • Gondola: Considered the best and largest of the fleet (Tested Fall 2023).
  • Legenda: A smaller and narrower version of the Gondola (Tested October 2025).
  • Duna Bella: Their most frequent daytime boat, but it is generally the least desirable choice, often due to its narrower upper deck.
  • The primary difference lies in their size and top deck, with some boats being larger and wider than others. However, even the smaller boats usually have plenty of space.
  • Unfortunately, Legenda does not allow you to select a specific boat unless you contact them directly and ask.

Gondola

  • The Gondola is a modern vessel that offers spacious seating, openable windows during the summer, a bar, an audio guide with headphones, and AC/heating.

Legenda / Danube Legend

  • The Legenda sightseeing boat is the younger sibling of the Gondola. It has the same equipment, but it is smaller and narrower.
  • A notable difference is that its audio guide equipment appeared to be better and more comfortable to use. Additionally, the upper deck offers rear-facing seats.

Duna Bella

  • The Duna Bella appears to be Legenda City Cruises' primary daytime sightseeing boat, although they use it for evening cruises as well. In my opinion, it is the least desirable of their fleet because of its narrow upper deck.

The Weather-Proof Waiting Area

  • Legenda is the only company in Budapest with a floating dock & waiting area that completely shields you from the elements.
  • The facilities include restrooms, one of which is accessible for those with reduced mobility, and a baby changing station.

Lower Deck

Ship: Gondola

  • The lower deck has a cozy bar at the back, near the stairs to the top deck. Rows of comfy seats offer plenty of room to move about, and the large windows guarantee fantastic views.

Ship: Legenda

  • The lower deck of the Legenda features a seating arrangement of two-by-three rows. The chairs themselves are quite comfortable. Each chair is equipped with its own audio guide headset.
  • The seating arrangement in the bow (front) of the boat is identical to the rest of the lower deck, but with one key difference: this section features a transparent glass roof, providing an open view.

The Upper Deck

Ship: Gondola

  • It's spacious with unobstructed 360° views from every angle. Ideal for photography and soaking in the city's beauty. Blankets are available upon request.
  • The interior lights are turned off during the all Legenda boats to minimize window glare. Some reflection from city streetlights may still occur.

Ship: Legenda

  • The upper deck features seating in two-by-two rows. The side windows can be slid open, allowing for fresh air and and eliminating any glare or reflection in photos.
  • Furthermore, unlike the Gondola, this section of the upper deck is covered.
  • Finally, the very back of the upper deck is an open-air section. From this area, passengers can admire the cityscape while seated and facing opposite the direction of travel.

The Audio Guide

Ship: Gondola

  • This was the only audio guide could actually be heard out of all the cruises, thanks to the headphones, as the other ships have loudspeakers.
  • The audio quality was excellent, and the narration was available in 30 languages.
  • There's room for improvement contentwise. Around half of the mentioned sights aren't relevant to riverside attractions. Plus, the content could dive deeper into the city's history, culture, and be more historically correct instead of telling stories, trivia and myths.
  • I encountered some issues with the headphones: the first set was defective, the second set had a cable that was too short, and the third set was uncomfortable and hard to wear.

Ship: Legenda

  • I was delighted to find that the audio guide on the Legenda was a significant improvement over the Gondola.
  • The headset was more comfortable, the cable length was longer, and the content quality was superior.
  • As expected, the audio guide is still available in 30 languages, and I had absolutely no complaints with the system.
💡 All the other cruises offer loudspeaker audio guides. Loudspeaker audio guides are often inaudible due to passenger noise. Other common issues include un-synced commentary, irrelevant sights/details mentioned, and infrequent audio.
