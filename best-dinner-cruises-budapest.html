6 Best Dinner Cruises in Budapest in 2026 (3 Personally Tested)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest River Cruise Specialist
Budapest Local • 6 Dinner Cruises Compared Side by Side • Updated 20 Nov 2026
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right Danube dinner cruise is the one that fits your evening: a quiet candlelit dinner for two, a lively Hungarian folk show, a buffet with unlimited drinks, or a cheap goulash supper on a sightseeing loop. This page gets you to that table in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I booked and ate on 3 of these dinner cruises myself (Legenda, Silverline and Hungária Koncert) and compared all 6 on what really matters: the menu, the show, drinks, seats, docks, sunset times and cancellation terms, checked on each seller's own page and in over 300 recent guest reviews. Departs at 19:00, back after 90 minutes. Arrive at least 15 minutes early.📍 Akadémia 2: Pest side, the second dock from the Chain Bridge towards Parliament. Look for the Gróf Széchenyi or Stadt Wien.
💃 Rajkó folk show▾
Musicians of the Rajkó Folk Orchestra play with a cimbalom, and folk dancers perform in costume. Guests say the band plays requests at the tables and the dancers come round for photos.
♿ Kids 6 and under free ▾
♿ Wheelchair: ⓘ not stated on the listing: ask the operator before you book🎟️ Kids: free for 6 and under (add them to the booking); child (7-12), student (13-25) and senior (66+) tickets
🥂 Welcome drink▾
1 welcome drink included. Other drinks are paid on board; a couple of guests suggest bringing change for tips.
🎉 Lively folk evening▾
A show night more than a sightseeing cruise: dinner, live Rajkó music and costumed dancers. 34 of 61 recent guests mention the dancers, 34 the music.
🌬️ Deck for the views▾
Guests say you can step out on deck to watch the lit-up city quietly. Guests who arrived 30 minutes early got a window table without booking one.
🍽️ 3 set menus▾
Served at your table: dips to start, then Urban Classic (goulash soup, pork tenderloin), Paprika Mood (chicken paprikash) or Veggie Flow (vegetable soup, pasta marinara), and Somlói sponge cake. Some past dates served a buffet instead: 6 of 61 recent guests mention one.
🚢 Historic ship▾
An evening cruise on a historic ship, the Gróf Széchenyi or Stadt Wien. One guest calls it a well-kept vintage boat.
✓ Pick it for a lively Hungarian evening with a 4-course dinner, Rajkó folk music and costumed dancers. ✕ Skip it if sightseeing is your priority: the show and the dinner take centre stage, and the views from the ship are limited.

Set menu or buffet?
The listing shows three set menus served at your table. 6 of 61 recent guests had a buffet on their date.
Can kids come?
Yes. Children 6 and under go free but must be added to the booking. Families say kids got to dance with the dancers.
How early should I arrive?
At least 15 minutes. Guests who came 30 minutes early got a window table without booking one.

🎭 3 show options▾
Dinner & Lights (no show, background music) from €77, or the Folk & Operetta Show with singers and folk dancers from €91. Same boat, same 4-course menu. Silverline also lists a Live Piano Show.
♿ No wheelchair access ▾
♿ Wheelchair: ✕ no wheelchair access🎟️ Kids: infant tickets don't include dinner (order on board); alcohol only from 18; service dogs only
🥂 Welcome drink▾
1 welcome drink included: Prosecco on Dinner & Lights, champagne on the folk show. Other drinks are paid on board, and you can't bring your own. 2 of 31 recent guests wish more were included.
🕯️ Calm, sunset start▾
A quiet dinner with background music on Dinner & Lights, timed to start around sunset. 18 of 31 recent guests praise the staff, many naming their waiter.
🪟 Window seat +€15▾
A window seat costs €15 more per person on Dinner & Lights, €25 on the Folk & Operetta show. Choose it when you book; one guest said the window table was worth it.
🍽️ 4-course menu▾
Panzanella with pear and burrata, then a choice of 2 soups, 4 mains (walnut cod, pork tenderloin, curry lamb chops or a vegetarian sweet potato boat) and 3 desserts. Same menu on every option.
🌱 Veg, GF & LF▾
A vegetarian main is on the menu, and most dishes are made with gluten-free and/or lactose-free ingredients. No allergen-free kitchen; tell Silverline about special diets in advance.
✓ Pick it for a good-value 4-course dinner with no show or a folk and operetta show, on the same boat. ✕ Skip it if you want drinks included or a window table at no extra cost: only one welcome drink is included and window seats cost more.

Yes, free up to 24 hours before departure when you book direct with Silverline. Within 24 hours there is no refund.
When should I arrive?
Boarding opens 45 minutes before the 19:00 departure. Arrive at least 30 minutes early.

HOW TO CHOOSE

How to Choose the Right Danube Dinner Cruise

Four things to decide before you book. Short on time? See the one dinner cruise I’d book.

Served Courses vs. Buffet

Legenda, Silverline, Hungária Koncert and the Dock 6 cruise serve plated courses at your table; River Diva is a seasonal buffet with unlimited drinks. When I tested Silverline, the food was prepared in the ship’s own kitchen.

Window Tables

Window tables are not guaranteed on most boats. Silverline sells them for +€15 (Dinner & Lights) or +€25 (Folk & Operetta); River Diva says tables follow booking size. On Hungária Koncert, boarding early gives you the best chance of a window.

Quiet Music or a Full Show

Decide between quiet conversation (Legenda’s live lounge music, Silverline’s background music) and a full show (Hungária Koncert’s Rajkó folk show, the Dock 6 folk dancers or Silverline’s Folk & Operetta evening).

⏱

Illumination Timing & Departure Docks

In autumn and winter, earlier 19:00 departures sail under full illumination. In summer (June–August), dusk does not settle until 20:45, making 20:30–21:00 the optimal booking window. Over the holidays, compare the Christmas cruises and New Year's Eve cruises in Budapest. Before boarding, walk the Budapest Christmas markets: Vörösmarty tér is a few minutes from the Pest piers.
