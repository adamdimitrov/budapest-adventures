9 Best Cooking Classes in Budapest in 2026 (Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
9 cooking classes compared on dishes cooked, hours, price per hour, group size and drinks
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right cooking class is the one that fits your day: a market walk and a Hungarian meal you cook yourself, goulash in a family kitchen, an hour baking chimney cake, or tasting your way around the city without cooking. This page gets you to that class in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared all 9 on what really decides it: how much you actually cook, hours, price per hour, group size and which drinks are poured, checked on each seller's own page. Full Hungarian Menu or Chimney Cake Workshop?

Full-menu classes take 2.5–4 hours and cost €89–145. You cook one to three savoury dishes (goulash, chicken paprikash, stuffed cabbage, Hortobágyi pancake) and usually a dessert, then eat it as lunch or dinner with wine. Treat it as a meal and an activity in one: the €89–145 replaces a restaurant bill.

Chimney cake workshops take 1–1.5 hours and cost €20–60. You make kürtőskalács, sweet dough wrapped round a spindle and baked until the sugar caramelises, and eat it warm or take it away. They are the better pick with children, on a short trip, or as a sweet stop between sights. If you want to master the dough, only #9 teaches it from scratch.

Before You Go: Diets, Getting There and What Costs Extra

  • Dietary needs: tell the host in advance. Chef Marti's one-dish class offers vegetarian, gluten-free, lactose-free and nut-free versions; the market walk class cannot accommodate coeliac disease; the Ipar utca chimney cake studio cannot remove wheat, milk, nuts, orange or coconut.

  • Minimum numbers: the from-scratch chimney cake class runs only with 8 participants, with a full refund if it does not.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best cooking class in Budapest?

For most visitors it is the market walk and Hungarian cooking class: €90 for 3–4 hours, a guided walk through the Central Market Hall, a main course and dessert cooked in an apartment kitchen, with wine pairing and pálinka included.

How much does a cooking class in Budapest cost?

Full Hungarian cooking classes cost €89–145 per person for 2.5–4 hours, about €22–38 an hour, usually including the meal and drinks. Chimney cake workshops cost €20–60 for 1–1.5 hours.

Where can I learn to cook goulash in Budapest?

The goulash and palacsinta class in a Buda family kitchen is built around it, including hand-pinched csipetke. Goulash is also menu B in Chef Marti's 3-course class, one of 4 choices in her one-dish class, and a menu option at Chefparade.

Where can I make chimney cake (kürtőskalács) in Budapest?

Three workshops: Ipar utca (€20, 1 cake, 65 minutes), Kató Néni near the Basilica (€33, 3 cakes, 1 hour) and Cooking Hungary (€60, 2 full-size cakes with dough made from scratch, 1.5 hours).

Which Budapest cooking classes include a market tour?

Two: the market walk and cooking class visits the Central Market Hall (€90), and Chefparade's market class tours a local market hall before a 3-course lunch (€145).

Are drinks included in Budapest cooking classes?

Yes, in all of them. The full-menu classes include wine, and three of them add pálinka; Chefparade's market class also pours Unicum. Chimney cake workshops include coffee, tea, water or other drinks.

Can children join a cooking class in Budapest?

The chimney cake workshops are the family pick: Ipar utca welcomes children with 1 adult per 4 kids, Kató Néni from age 5 and the from-scratch class from age 8. Chef Marti's 3-course class is not suitable for under-10s.
