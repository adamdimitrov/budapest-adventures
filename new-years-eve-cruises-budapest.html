Summary & Review Overview ▾

Silverline is the only operator this year with three clearly different New Year's Eve programs, so you can pick your style and budget and still book with one company.

The Gala Dinner (€340) is the calm, elegant option: boarding at 20:00, a 5-course menu where you choose each course, live piano, and a sparkling-wine toast right in front of the illuminated Parliament at midnight. The Celebration Party (€239) is the opposite: a DJ, tapas instead of a sit-down dinner, and unlimited prosecco, wine and beer from 22:00 to 01:00.

The NYE Booze Cruise is the cheapest way to be on the water in Budapest at midnight: unlimited wine, sparkling wine, draught beer and soft drinks, no food. Take the Midnight sailing (23:00, 90 minutes, €125) to see the new year in on the Danube, or a 60-minute evening sailing (18:30, 20:00 or 21:30, €70) as a warm-up before a party on land. On any other evening, see my ranking of the best night cruises in Budapest.

💡 Tip: Embankments get crowded and partly closed on New Year's Eve, so get to the pier at least 30 minutes before boarding, and plan your way home after 01:00 in advance.
