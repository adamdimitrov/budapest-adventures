By Adam Dimitrov — Founder & Local Budapest Tester
Programs & prices from operators and GetYourGuide • Independent comparison
Last updated: Nov 10, 2026

Want to ring in 2027 on the Danube, between the Parliament and Buda Castle? New Year's Eve is the busiest night of the year on the river, and the good boats sell out weeks in advance.

I compared every New Year's Eve cruise you can actually book this year — from a €164 cruise-and-party to an €820 gala package — and checked what's really included: how long you're actually on the water, food, drinks, and where you'll be at midnight.

Programs and prices come directly from the operators and GetYourGuide (last checked: Nov 10, 2026).

🏆 #1 Best Overall NYE Cruise
Silverline Cruises • ⏱️ 3–5 Hours • Dock 11

Silverline New Year's Eve Cruises — Gala Dinner or Celebration Party

From €239
▾

Pros:

  • Two programs, one operator — seated gala or party, depending on your crowd.

Summary & Review Overview ▾

Silverline is the only operator this year with two clearly different New Year's Eve programs, so you can pick your style and still book with one company.

The Gala Dinner (€340) is the calm, elegant option: boarding at 20:00, a 5-course menu where you choose each course, live piano, and a sparkling-wine toast right in front of the illuminated Parliament at midnight. The Celebration Party (€239) is the opposite: a DJ, tapas instead of a sit-down dinner, and unlimited prosecco, wine and beer from 22:00 to 01:00.

💡 Tip: Embankments get crowded and partly closed on New Year's Eve — get to the pier at least 30 minutes before boarding, and plan your way home after 01:00 in advance.

New Year's Eve Cruise With Unlimited Wine, Champagne & Beer

Summary & Review Overview ▾

Portum's New Year's Eve cruise is the best value if drinks matter more than dinner. There are two options: Food & Drinks (22:00, 4 hours, cold & hot snack buffet with a vegan option) and Only Drinks (23:00, 2 hours). Both include unlimited local beer, wine, mulled wine, champagne and soft drinks. Portum itself describes it as a simple, informal party — not an elegant gala.

The one downside is the pier: Dock 42 at Szent István park is north of the centre, so plan your trip there and back.

💡 Tip: Embankments get crowded and partly closed on New Year's Eve — get to the pier at least 30 minutes before boarding, and plan your way home after 01:00 in advance.
