The 7 Best Hop-On Hop-Off and Sightseeing Bus Tours in Budapest in 2026 (Prices Compared)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder, Budapest Adventures
7 Bus Tours Compared Side by Side
Last updated: Nov 15, 2026

Quick answer: book the City Sightseeing 48-hour ticket (€44 with a boat ride and free cancellation), or skip the open-top bus and ride tram 2 and bus 16 on a €7 day pass.

Budapest's two big hop-on hop-off companies charge exactly the same: €39 for one day, €44 for two, €49 for three. The difference is in the small print. One ticket includes a Danube boat ride and free cancellation; the other is non-refundable. And the second day costs just €5 more, which is why the 24-hour ticket is the worst value of the three.

Below are the 7 best bus tours in Budapest side by side, from the classic open-top loops to a bus that splashes into the Danube, an electric shuttle up Castle Hill and two party buses with beer on tap. For each one: price per day, stops, how often the buses come, what's included and who should skip it.

Which Budapest Bus Tour Should You Book?

  • 2 or 3 days, want the classic open-top loop: City Sightseeing (48 hours €44, boat ride and walking tour included, free cancellation).
  • Dates fixed, want the most stops: Big Bus (21 stops, same prices, river cruise included, non-refundable).
  • Kids, or one ride on land and water: RiverRide amphibious bus (90 minutes €32, splashes into the Danube).
  • Castle Hill without the climb: Buda Castle electric bus (€18, 5 stops from the Chain Bridge).
  • One morning and a real guide: 3-hour live guided tour (€35, Castle District walk included).
  • A fun hour with a drink: BeerBus (€27, unlimited beer, 5 departures a day).
  • Stag, hen or birthday group: E-BeerBus (from €27 each, private bus from €360, your own music).
  • Tight budget: no tour at all. A 24-hour public transport pass is 2,750 HUF (about €7); see the routes below.

24, 48 or 72 Hours: Which Ticket Is Worth It?

The buses run about 7 to 8 hours a day, and a full loop with commentary takes close to 2 hours. That makes the longer tickets far better value:

Go for 48 hours if you are in Budapest for 2 nights or more. Day one: ride the full loop with the commentary and do the walking tour and the boat. Day two: use the bus as transport to Heroes' Square, Széchenyi Bath and the Castle Garden. The 72-hour ticket only pays off if you will really ride on day three; by then most people know the city well enough to use the metro.

Compare that with the fixed tours: the RiverRide costs about €21 per hour, the guided coach tour about €12 per hour and the BeerBus €27 for its hour. You pay for the experience, not the transport.

Is a Hop-On Hop-Off Bus Worth It? The €7 Alternative

Budapest's public transport passes most of the same sights for a fraction of the price. A 24-hour travelcard is 2,750 HUF (about €7) and a 72-hour travelcard 5,750 HUF (under €15), valid on every metro, tram and bus in the city.

  • Tram 2 runs along the Pest bank of the Danube past the Parliament, with views of Buda Castle across the river. Often called one of the most scenic tram rides in Europe.
  • Bus 16 goes from Deák Ferenc tér over the Chain Bridge and up to Castle Hill, the same climb the €18 castle bus saves you.
  • Bus 105 also crosses the Chain Bridge between Pest and Buda.
  • Metro M1, the oldest line on the continent, runs under Andrássy Avenue from the Opera to Heroes' Square and City Park.

Choose public transport if you are comfortable finding your own way and don't need commentary. Choose the hop-on bus if you want the stories behind the buildings in your language, an open top deck for photos, a guided walk and a boat ride on the same ticket, or you are travelling with kids or older family who need a seat between sights. Planning to visit several museums too? The Budapest Card includes unlimited public transport.

❓ Is City Sightseeing better than Big Bus in Budapest?
💡 For most visitors, yes, by a small margin: the same adult prices, but the boat ride is included, cancellation is free up to 2 days before and the child price runs to age 18.

City Sightseeing runs open-top double-deckers on a 20-stop red route that covers both sides of the river: St Stephen's Basilica, the Opera on Andrássy Avenue, Heroes' Square, the Dohány Street Synagogue, Gellért Square, the Castle Garden by the funicular, Batthyány Square and the Parliament. A full loop takes about 110 minutes.

The ticket is the best-value package in town: the bus, a Danube boat ride and a 1-hour guided walking tour through the streets the bus can't reach. At €39 for 24 hours the boat alone covers a good part of the price, because a 1-hour sightseeing cruise costs about €11 to €20 on its own (compare them in our Budapest river cruise guide).

Two stops (both at Astoria) are temporarily closed, so use Dohány Street or the Opera instead.

❓ Is the Big Bus worth it in Budapest?
💡 Yes for a 2 or 3 day ticket, when the cruise, the walk and the extra days bring the cost down to about €16 to €22 a day. For a single afternoon, public transport is better value.

Big Bus has the biggest network of the classic operators: a 21-stop red route from the Parliament and Margaret Bridge through the Opera and Heroes' Square to Keleti station, Astoria, the Gellért Baths and Buda Castle. Buses run from 10:00 to 17:00, every 10 to 25 minutes.

This ticket bundles a Danube river cruise and a guided walking tour of Pest, so you get the city from the top deck, the water and on foot. The 2-day Essential ticket at €44 is the sweet spot: only €5 more than one day.

Want to keep your options open? The flexible Big Bus ticket can be cancelled up to 24 hours before, at the same price. It gives you 30% off a cruise at the pier instead of a free one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best hop-on hop-off bus in Budapest?

City Sightseeing, by a small margin over Big Bus. Both cost €39 for 24 hours, €44 for 48 and €49 for 72, but City Sightseeing includes a boat ride, can be cancelled for free up to 2 days before and charges the child price up to age 18.

How much is the hop-on hop-off bus in Budapest?

€39 for 24 hours, €44 for 48 hours and €49 for 72 hours per adult on both Big Bus and City Sightseeing. Children pay €35.10, €39.60 and €44.10; under-6s ride free on City Sightseeing. Some dates are discounted to €33.

Is the hop-on hop-off bus in Budapest worth it?

Yes for a 48-hour ticket if you want commentary, a boat ride and a walking tour in one price (€22 a day). If you only want to get around, a 24-hour public transport pass at 2,750 HUF (about €7) is far cheaper: tram 2 and bus 16 pass many of the same sights.

What time does the hop-on hop-off bus start in Budapest?

City Sightseeing runs from 9:00 to 17:00 every 10 to 20 minutes, Big Bus from 10:00 to 17:00 every 10 to 25 minutes. The last departure from stop 1 is at 17:00.

Does the Budapest hop-on hop-off include a river cruise?

Yes, both the City Sightseeing ticket and the Big Bus ticket in this guide include a Danube boat ride. Big Bus's flexible, cancellable version gives you 30% off a cruise at the pier instead.

How do you get up to Buda Castle without walking?

Take the €18 official Buda Castle electric bus from Clark Ádám tér, which stops at 5 points on Castle Hill, or public bus 16 from Deák Ferenc tér on a normal ticket.

Is there a bus that goes into the Danube?

Yes, the RiverRide amphibious bus drives through Pest and then splashes into the Danube. The 50-minute tour is from €27; the 90-minute tour (€32) sails to the Parliament.

Is there a beer bus in Budapest?

Two. The BeerBus is €27 per person for an hour with unlimited beer and 5 departures a day. The electric E-BeerBus is from €27 per person for groups of 4 or more, or from €360 for a private bus with your own music.
