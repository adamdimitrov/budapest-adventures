8 Best Things to Do in Budapest in November 2026 (Night Cruises, Hot Pools and Market Openings)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
8 Picks • 7 Personally Tested • Dates & Daylight
Last updated: Nov 19, 2026

The right November evening is the one that fits your trip: a cruise past the lit-up Parliament, a hot outdoor pool in the cold, a dinner cruise, or a night walk in the Castle District. This page gets you to that plan in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I did 7 of these 8 myself and ranked all 8 for a November trip on price, timing after dark and what is really included, checked on each seller's own page, plus the dates that change your plans and the free evenings. Filter by the Danube, baths, after dark, food or under €40 below, or scroll straight to the ranking.

November Weather and Daylight in Budapest

Expect around 9 °C by day and 3 °C at night, a monthly average of 5.9 °C and about 48 mm of rain (HungaroMet, 1991-2020 averages). It is grey more often than not, and it gets colder through the month. Pack a warm waterproof jacket and shoes with grip for the cobbles on Castle Hill.

How to use the short days: outdoor sights and the market hall from 9:00 to 14:00, a bath at dusk, then a cruise, dinner or a night walk after 16:30.

Christmas Markets in November

You don't have to wait for December. The two big central markets opened on 13 November, and the Castle Hill and Óbuda markets open on 27 November. All four are free to enter. The Basilica facade gets a 3D light show every 30 minutes from 17:30.

Free Things to Do in November

  • Walk the river after dark. From the Pest embankment between the Chain Bridge and the Parliament you get the lit Castle, the bridges and the Parliament for free, from about 16:30.
  • Walk the Christmas markets. Advent Bazilika and Vörösmarty tér are free to enter and five minutes apart, open daily from 11:00. Watch the Basilica light show from 17:30.
  • Climb Castle Hill at night. The Castle District and the lower terraces of Fisherman's Bastion are free and almost empty after dark.
  • Skate in Óbuda. The rink on Óbuda's Fő tér is free from 27 November (Sunday to Thursday 8:00 to 21:00, Friday and Saturday to 22:00).
  • Art Weekend Budapest. On 27 to 29 November galleries and studios across the city open for free.

Cons:

Summary & Review Overview ▾

November is the month the Danube cruise gets better, not worse. The sun sets at 16:07 in mid-November, so Legenda's first evening boat at 16:20 already sails past a lit Parliament and Buda Castle, and you are back on the embankment before 17:30 with the whole evening ahead.

I've ridden this cruise myself. It is a one-hour loop with a welcome drink and the best audio guide on the river: a film and commentary on your own headset in 30 languages. From 23 November Legenda adds a 15:40 sailing that catches the last light.

Who Should Book It

Pick it as your first evening in Budapest: it shows you the city lit up in an hour and leaves the night free for dinner.

Skip it if you want to eat on the water: book Legenda's candlelit dinner instead, from the same dock. All night cruises side by side: best night cruises in Budapest.

Meeting Point

🗺️ Meeting Point: Legenda, Jane Haining rakpart 7, Dock 7 (Vigadó tér), Pest side.
🚋 Public Transport: Tram 2 to Vigadó tér, or M1 to Vörösmarty tér.

#2 Best Bath in the Cold

Széchenyi Thermal Bath

4.332.7k+ reviews · 3 sites▾

Pros:

  • Three outdoor pools open in November, the hottest at 36 to 38 °C
  • Open until 22:00 on Fridays, after dark from 16:00
  • Metro stop at the entrance

Cons:

  • The busiest bath in town
  • Booking-site tickets cost more than the desk
Summary & Review Overview ▾

Cold air is what makes Széchenyi. In November it is around 9 °C by day and 3 °C at night, and the outdoor thermal pool is 36 to 38 °C, so the steam rises off the water under the yellow neo-baroque facade. Come at about 15:30 and you swim from daylight into dusk.

I've been to Széchenyi myself. On Fridays it stays open until 22:00, the best night of the week for a bath after dark.

Who Should Book It

Pick it for your first Budapest bath. The €39 morning pass (Mon to Thu, entry by 9:00) is the best value.

Skip it if you want something calmer and older: book Rudas. All six baths compared: best thermal baths in Budapest.

Getting There

🚇 Metro: M1 to Széchenyi fürdő, the station is at the entrance.
🎒 Bring: Swimsuit, flip-flops and a towel. Walk fast from the indoor halls to the outdoor pools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Budapest worth visiting in November?

Yes, if you plan around the dark. Days are short (sunset around 16:00) and often grey at about 9 °C, but the city is lit up early, the thermal baths' outdoor pools are at their best in cold air, and from 13 November the central Christmas markets are open.

What is the weather like in Budapest in November?

Cool and often grey: about 9 °C by day and 3 °C at night, with around 48 mm of rain in the month (1991-2020 averages). The sun rises between 6:27 and 7:09 and sets between 16:26 and 15:55 over the month.

Are the Budapest Christmas markets open in November 2026?

Yes. Advent Bazilika (Szent István tér) and Vörösmarty tér opened on 13 November 2026. Budavári Advent on Castle Hill and Advent Óbudán open on 27 November. All are free to enter. Details: Budapest Christmas markets 2026.

Are the thermal baths open in November?

Yes, all year. Széchenyi's outdoor pools stay open (the thermal pool is 36 to 38 °C); it is open 7:00 to 20:00 Monday to Thursday, until 22:00 on Friday and 8:00 to 20:00 at weekends. Rudas has night bathing on Friday and Saturday from 22:00 to 3:00.

Do Danube cruises run in November?

Yes. Legenda's evening cruise sails every 20 minutes from 16:20 to 21:00, with an extra 15:40 boat from 23 November. Dinner cruises sail at 19:00, such as Legenda's candlelit dinner (€110) and Silverline's Dinner & Lights (from €77).

What can I do in Budapest on a rainy November day?

Go to a thermal bath (Rudas's rooftop pool is warm in any weather), take the Foodapest market tour, which is mostly indoors, or see the Gauguin exhibition at the Museum of Fine Arts (Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:00).

When does the City Park Ice Rink open in 2026?

The rink plans to open on 20 November 2026. The date can move with the weather, so check mujegpalya.hu before you go. Óbuda's smaller rink on Fő tér is free from 27 November.

What is there to do in Budapest in the evening in November?

It is dark from about 16:30, so evenings are long: a one-hour night cruise, a bath after dark (Széchenyi until 22:00 on Fridays), the vampire night walk at 20:00, the Christmas markets until 21:00 or later, or Lumina Park on Margaret Island.
