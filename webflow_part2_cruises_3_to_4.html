Summary: Silverline's Folk Operetta Experience

Silverline's Folk Operetta Dinner Cruise combines a traditional sit-down 4-course meal with high-spirited Hungarian cultural entertainment. The performers change into colorful hand-embroidered heritage costumes, executing intricate Hungarian csárdás dances between courses.

🛳️ Key Differences Among Silverline Dinner Cruises

Silverline operates the same catamaran hull for three distinct dinner products:

  • No-Show (€74): Upper deck seating, quiet background music, lowest price.
  • Folk Operetta Show (€91): Lower deck main salon, live dancers, traditional Hungarian costumes and violin.
  • Piano Battle Show (€91): Lower deck main salon, dual grand pianos, musical comedy, and guest vocalist.
