Which Budapest Pub Crawl Should You Book?

  • Solo, first night, want the biggest party: Pub Crawl & Ruin Bar Tour (from €2, 4-5 venues, a shot at each).
  • Booking on reviews alone: Ruin Bars Pub Crawl (from €2.18, rated 5.0 from 1,657 reviews).
  • Dinner first, crawl later: Party Deck (22:00 start, 5-6 venues).
  • Want to meet in a pub, not on a corner: Original Europe Tours (€19, Becketts Irish Bar, 21:30).
  • Good drinks over cheap shots: Hidden Bars and Cocktails crawl (€25, 5 drinks included, 20:00 to 23:00).
  • Stag or hen group of 8+: Private pub crawl with pickup (from €27 per person).
  • Group of 6 to 12, normal night out: the private version of #4 (€100 for the whole group on Friday 20 November).

What a €2 Ticket Really Covers (and What €25 Adds)

The €2 crawls aren't a trick, but read them as a guide, a group and a queue-skip, not a drinks package. Across #1 to #3, the price covers a welcome shot (at every venue on #1, at the first bar and the club on #2), drinking games, photos and VIP fast-track entry at the club, usually Instant-Fogas. Every beer, cocktail and glass of wine after that is bought at the bar. Food is never included.

The €2 price also moves by date. On Friday 20 November 2026, #1 was €8.50, #2 was €2.18 or €4 depending on the start time, and #3 was €2 or €6. Still cheap, but check your own date before you plan around €2.

At €25, the hidden bars crawl flips the deal: 5 drinks are included (pálinka, a tour cocktail, fröccs or beer, two shots), but there's no club and it ends at 23:00. If you'd drink four or five rounds anyway, that's where the €23 difference goes.

Which Ruin Bars and Clubs Are on the Route?

Routes rotate with opening hours and crowds, but the venue lists overlap heavily. Instant-Fogas, the huge multi-room ruin club on Akácfa utca, gets VIP entry on #1, #3 and #4, and #2 ends at Instant too. Füge Udvar, La Siesta, Doboz and Humbák show up on almost every list.

Szimpla Kert, the ruin bar on every postcard, is on none of the venue lists for #1 to #4. It's free to walk into, so the easy fix is to go there first, around 20:00 when you can still move, then join a 21:30 or 22:00 crawl a few streets away.

Free Self-Guided Ruin Bar Route vs a Paid Crawl

You don't need a tour to see the ruin bars: the best ones sit within a 10-minute walk of each other in District VII. A simple free route: Szimpla Kert (Kazinczy utca 14) for the famous courtyards, then Doboz (Klauzál utca 10), and finish at Instant-Fogas (Akácfa utca 49-51) if you want to dance.

Hungry first? The Jewish Quarter tour in our best food tours in Budapest ends on Kazinczy utca, the same street as Szimpla, so dinner and the first ruin bar are a two-minute walk apart.

Go self-guided if you're a couple who wants to look around, have two drinks and be in bed by midnight. Book a crawl if you're solo, want a group to go out with, or it's a Friday or Saturday and you'd rather skip the club queue: at €2, the ticket costs less than a beer in Szimpla.

Making a whole evening of it? Start on the river with an unlimited prosecco cruise or one of the best night cruises, then walk 15 minutes into District VII for a 21:30 or 22:00 crawl.

Same Crawl, Two Listings: What We Left Out

Several Budapest crawls are sold more than once under different names. Ruin Bar Crawl, Karaoke, Free Shots & VIP Entry shares #1's meeting point (Erzsébet körút 36), its 21:10 and 22:20 start times, its venues and even the guides named in reviews. Pub Crawl & Ruin Bar Tour with 6 Shots and Social Ruin Pub Tour start from #3's meeting point at JO&JOE with the same house rules. We kept the listing with the most reviews for each; if a twin is cheaper on your date, it's the same night out.

Before You Go: Rules, ID and Getting Home

  • 18+ only, bring ID. Every crawl here asks for a passport or ID card; most accept a photo copy.
  • No drinking on the street. Drinking outside, shouting or public urination can mean fines of up to €400. Crawls keep it inside the bars.
  • Arrive sober-ish. Party Deck turns away anyone visibly drunk at check-in, and the others ban intoxication too.
  • Pay at the bar. Beyond what's listed as included, you buy your own drinks, so carry a card or some forint.
  • Getting home: tram 6 runs all night along the Grand Boulevard (Erzsébet körút), a few minutes' walk from every finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a pub crawl in Budapest cost?

The big party crawls start at about €2 (€2 to €8.50 on a Friday), which covers the guide, shots, games and VIP club entry. A crawl with 5 drinks included costs about €25, and a private stag crawl starts at €27 per person for 8 or more.

What is included in a €2 pub crawl in Budapest?

A guide, a welcome shot (on some crawls one at every venue), drinking games, photos and fast-track entry to the club at the end, usually Instant-Fogas. Every other drink and all food you pay for at the bar.

What time do Budapest pub crawls start and finish?

Most start between 21:00 and 22:20 and run 5 to 6 hours, so the organised part ends around 02:00 to 03:30 in a club you can stay in. The hidden bars and cocktails crawl starts at 20:00 and ends around 23:00.

Do Budapest pub crawls go to Szimpla Kert?

Not usually. Szimpla Kert isn't on the venue lists of the four biggest crawls, which favour Instant-Fogas, Füge Udvar, La Siesta, Doboz and Humbák. Szimpla is free to enter, so visit it first and join a crawl afterwards.

Are pub crawls in Budapest good for solo travellers?

Yes, that's who they're built for. The €2 crawls start with icebreakers and drinking games at the first bar, and reviewers who came alone write about the friends they made.

Is there an age limit on Budapest pub crawls?

All of them are 18+ and ask for ID (most accept a copy). Two set upper limits too: the Liszt Ferenc tér crawl is for under-70s and the hidden bars crawl for under-65s.

Can I visit the ruin bars without a tour?

Yes. Szimpla Kert (Kazinczy utca 14), Doboz (Klauzál utca 10) and Instant-Fogas (Akácfa utca 49-51) are all within a 10-minute walk in District VII. A crawl mainly adds a group, shots and a skip-the-queue entry at the club on busy nights.

Which Budapest pub crawl is best for a stag or hen party?

For 8 or more, the private pub crawl with pickup (from €27 per person, 4 rounds of shots, nightclub or strip club entry). For 6 to 12 who want a normal crawl to themselves, the private version from Liszt Ferenc tér was €100 per group on Friday 20 November.

Is there a New Year's Eve pub crawl in Budapest?

Yes. Party Deck runs a NYE night with 2 hours of unlimited drinks and VIP entry at Instant-Fogas, and the Liszt Ferenc tér crawl adds a 1-hour open bar on 31 December. Prefer the fireworks from the river? See our New Year's Eve cruises in Budapest.
