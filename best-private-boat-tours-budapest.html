Summary & Review Overview ▾
❓ Can two people book a whole private boat in Budapest?
💡 Yes. The price is per boat, not per person, so a couple pays the same €225 as a group of 20. Guests regularly book it for two.

Portum Lines' 1-hour private cruise is the best value private boat in Budapest: you get a whole motor boat, the Budapest Klára, with its crew for €225 on weekdays (€275 on the Saturday we checked).

The boat glides slowly through the city centre between Margaret Bridge and Elisabeth Bridge, passing Parliament, Buda Castle and the Chain Bridge. It has a sound system and a bar, but no guide and no drinks included.

Guests on GetYourGuide rate it 4.9 out of 5 from 52 reviews. They mention a friendly, flexible crew and the feeling of having the river to themselves. The most common complaint is that one hour feels short.

How to Get There

  • 📍 Meeting point: Dock 42 port, Szent István park, 1138 Budapest
  • ❌ The dock is north of Margaret Bridge, a bit out of the way compared to the central piers.
  • 🗺️ Take tram 2, 4 or 6 to Jászai Mari tér, then walk about 500 metres north along the river until you see the Dock 42 sign.

Dunarama's water limousine is the most stylish private boat in Budapest: a polished wooden boat in Venetian style for up to 10 guests, with a glass of Hungarian sparkling wine each.

Portum's party boat is the easiest private stag or hen do on the Danube: one price of €450 on weekdays covers the boat for up to 20 people and unlimited beer, wine and sparkling wine for an hour.

The boat is a refitted Dutch vessel with a bar, sound system, neon party lights and heating, and it leaves from the same Dock 42 as the Portum private boat. Most groups carry on in the ruin bars afterwards: our best pub crawls in Budapest include a private stag crawl with pickup for 8 or more.

Only 16 people have reviewed it so far: 5.0 out of 5 on GetYourGuide (10) and 3.5 on Viator (6). The one concrete complaint is warm beer once the cold cans ran out.

How to Get There

  • 📍 Meeting point: Dock 42 port, Szent István park, 1138 Budapest
  • ❌ The dock is north of Margaret Bridge, a bit out of the way compared to the central piers.
  • 🗺️ Take tram 2, 4 or 6 to Jászai Mari tér, then walk about 500 metres north along the river until you see the Dock 42 sign.
