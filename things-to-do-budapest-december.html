11 Best Things to Do in Budapest in December 2026 (Christmas, Baths and Holiday Hours)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
11 Picks • 4 Personally Tested • Holiday Hours
Last updated: Nov 21, 2026

The right December plan is the one that fits your trip: a candlelit dinner on the Danube, a hot outdoor pool after dark, an evening at the Christmas markets, or New Year's Eve on the river. This page gets you to that plan in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I did 4 of these 11 myself and ranked all 11 for a winter trip on price, what runs over Christmas and what is really included, checked on each seller's own page, plus the holiday opening hours and the Nutcracker dates. What Is Open on 24-26 and 31 December

Hungary made 24 December a public holiday on 3 November 2026, from this year on. Shops must stay closed that day, except restaurants, hotels, flower and sweet shops, petrol stations and shops at stations and the airport. 25 and 26 December are public holidays as usual. December Weather and Daylight in Budapest

Expect around 5 °C by day and just below 0 °C at night, with about 7 days of rain or snow in the month and only about 49 hours of sunshine (1991-2020 averages). Snow on the ground is possible but not the rule. Pack a warm coat, a hat and shoes with grip for the cobbles on Castle Hill.

The Nutcracker at the Opera

The Hungarian State Opera stages The Nutcracker almost every day in December: 28 performances between 1 and 31 December 2026, including 11:00 on Christmas Eve and two on New Year's Eve (13:00 and 21:00). Tickets are sold on the Opera's own site, jegy.opera.hu, and the weekend and holiday shows go first. No ticket? The 60-minute Opera House tour in English runs daily at 13:30, 15:00 and 16:30 when there is no rehearsal.

Free Things to Do in December

  • Walk the two central Christmas markets. Advent Bazilika (Szent István tér) and Vörösmarty tér are free to enter and five minutes apart. The Basilica facade gets a 3D light show every 30 minutes from 17:30. Hours, prices and a walking route: Budapest Christmas markets 2026.
  • Skate on Óbuda's free rink. The rink on Óbuda's Fő tér is free from 27 November to 31 January (Sun to Thu 8:00 to 21:00, Fri and Sat to 22:00). The big City Park Ice Rink in front of Vajdahunyad Castle plans to open on 20 November; tickets are sold on its own site.
  • See the Advent candles on Castle Hill. On each Advent Sunday (29 November, 6, 13 and 20 December) a candle is lit at 16:00 on Szentháromság tér, by the Budavári Advent market.
  • Watch the light trams. BKV runs trams and boats covered in lights every Advent. In 2025 they ran daily from 28 November to 6 January from about 16:00, except on 24 and 31 December; the 2026 timetable is announced on bkv.hu.
  • Walk the river after dark. From the Pest embankment between the Chain Bridge and the Parliament you get the lit Castle, the bridges and the Parliament for free, from about 16:30.

Pros:

  • Served 4-course dinner you order at the table, not a buffet
  • Sails at 19:00 every evening to 31 December, Christmas Day included
  • Glass-roofed boat: you see the lit-up Parliament from your seat

Cons:

  • Drinks are extra on board
  • No dinner cruise on 24 December, and no Christmas-themed menu
Summary & Review Overview ▾

December is when a Danube dinner makes the most sense: it is dark by 16:00, the bridges and the Parliament are lit for hours, and the boat is warm. Legenda's candlelit cruise is the one I'd book. It is a served 4-course à la carte dinner with live music on a glass-roofed boat, and it sails every evening at 19:00 until 31 December, except Christmas Eve.

I've taken this cruise myself. What sets it apart from the buffet boats is the service: you order each course at your table, the pace is slow.

Who Should Book It

Pick it for a date night or a calm family dinner on any December evening, including 25 and 26 December. Kids 3 to 12 eat from a 3-course menu.

Skip it if you want an all-inclusive price: drinks are paid on board. For a menu written for Christmas Day, see Silverline's 25 December dinner.

Meeting Point

🗺️ Meeting Point: Legenda pier, Dock 7, Vigadó tér (Pest side).
🚇 Public Transport: Vörösmarty tér (M1), tram 2 to Vigadó tér.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Budapest worth visiting in December?

Yes. December is one of the best months for Budapest: the Christmas markets run all month, the outdoor thermal pools are at their best in the cold, and the city is lit up from 16:00. Expect around 5 °C by day and short days, so plan sights in the morning and evenings for the markets, baths and river.

What is open in Budapest on Christmas Eve?

From 2026, 24 December is a public holiday and shops are closed, except restaurants, hotels, flower and sweet shops and petrol stations. The Advent Bazilika market opens 11:00 to 15:00 and Vörösmarty tér 10:00 to 14:00. In the evening, the Hungária Koncert Christmas Eve cruise sails at 19:00 and 21:30. Restaurants and hotels may open, but Legenda's dinner cruise does not sail on 24 December.

Are the thermal baths open at Christmas?

Yes. Széchenyi opens at 10:00 on 25 and 26 December and 1 January, and at 8:00 from 18 December to 6 January. On 8 December it closes at 16:00. Rudas has night bathing on Friday and Saturday from 22:00 to 3:00.

Is there a Christmas dinner cruise on 25 December?

Yes. Silverline's Christmas Day dinner cruise (€155) sails on 25 December from 19:30 to 22:30 with a 4-course festive menu and live piano. Legenda's candlelit dinner (€110) also sails every evening in December except Christmas Eve, 25 December included.

Can I take a Christmas river cruise from Budapest to Vienna?

Yes. Emerald (5 days) and Avalon (6 days) sail one way from Budapest to Vienna in late November and December, with guided tours and Christmas markets in Budapest, Bratislava and Vienna. The cheapest option is a 5-day loop from Vienna via Budapest from $859. All six are compared in the best Budapest to Vienna river cruises.

When do the Budapest Christmas markets close?

Advent Bazilika runs until 1 January 2027 and Vörösmarty tér until 31 December (its craft stalls until 28 December). Both stay open until 3:00 on New Year's Eve. The Óbuda market closes on 23 December, and Budavári Advent runs until 3 January.

What is the weather like in Budapest in December?

Cold and often grey: about 5 °C by day and just below freezing at night, around 7 days of rain or snow and 49 hours of sunshine in the month. The sun rises around 7:20 and sets just before 16:00.

Is the Nutcracker on in Budapest in December 2026?

Yes. The Hungarian State Opera has 28 Nutcracker performances from 1 to 31 December 2026, including 11:00 on 24 December and 13:00 and 21:00 on 31 December. Tickets are sold on jegy.opera.hu.

What can I do in Budapest on New Year's Eve?

The two central Christmas markets stay open until 3:00, the Opera plays the Nutcracker at 21:00, and party cruises sail at midnight, such as Silverline's New Year's Eve cruise (22:00 to 1:00, €239). More options: New Year's Eve cruises in Budapest.
