Which Rudas Ticket Should You Book?

  • Bath plus lunch or dinner, free cancellation: Day Pass + 3-Course Meal (from €74.99 Mon–Thu, €129.90 at weekends).
  • The full bath for the least money: All-Zones Day Ticket (12,000 HUF at the desk Mon–Thu, fast track online from €45).
  • An evening plan, co-ed Turkish bath: Night Bathing (Fri and Sat 22:00 to 3:00, €45–54 online).
  • Just the Turkish bath, on a budget: Single-Zone Ticket (8,000 HUF or €24).
  • A proposal or anniversary: Private Rooftop Hour (€117–147 per person, 20:00–21:00).

Summary & Review Overview Overview ▾
❓ Is the Rudas meal package worth it?
💡 Yes, if you would eat at Rudas Bistro anyway. If you only want to bathe, the official desk ticket is cheaper.

This is the ticket for a slow Rudas day: soak in the wellness pools and the 36 °C rooftop pool, then sit down for a 3-course lunch or dinner at Rudas Bistro, which looks over the Danube and Elizabeth Bridge. On weekdays the cheapest option is €74.99 with coffee; pick an option with Turkish bath in the name if you want the Ottoman pools too.

Guests love the food and the rooftop view. The recurring complaints are crowds on the rooftop and a layout that is hard to find your way around: the wellness area, the Turkish bath and the roof are separate sections.

❓ Is it cheaper to buy Rudas tickets at the desk?
💡 Yes. The desk price is 12,000 HUF Mon–Thu for all zones. Online fast-track tickets cost a little more but fix your entry window and skip the queue.

This is the ticket I would buy for a first visit without lunch plans. It covers all three parts of Rudas: the Ottoman Turkish bath, the wellness area with the sauna world and the 36 °C rooftop panorama pool, plus the 29 °C swimming pool.

My advice from my own visit: go on a Monday, Thursday or Friday after 11:00 for the co-ed Turkish bath, or on a weekend morning with the 8:00 fast-track ticket. The rooftop pool holds only 16 people, so it is far nicer before the midday crowd.

Rudas Baths: Know Before You Go

What to Bring

Swimsuit, towel and flip-flops (mandatory at night), plus a swim cap if you want the swimming pool. None of the tickets include a towel. At the desk: towel 6,900 HUF, bathrobe 12,600 HUF, slippers 4,200 HUF, swim cap 2,100 HUF, swimsuit 6,300 HUF.

Locker or Cabin?

Every ticket includes a locker in a shared changing room, opened with a wristband (3,000 HUF if lost). A private cabin costs 1,000 HUF extra: worth it if you are two and want to change in private.

Rooftop Pool

The 36 °C panorama pool fits 16 people. It fills up from late morning, so go early (weekend fast track from 8:00) or at night.

Age Limit and Access

Rudas does not admit children under 14, at any time. The bath is not step-free: for technical reasons it currently cannot receive guests with reduced mobility.

Getting There

Rudas is at Döbrentei tér 9, at the Buda end of Elizabeth Bridge. Take tram 19, 41, 56 or 56A, or bus 7, 8E, 108E, 110 or 112. From the Pest side it is a 10-minute walk across Elizabeth Bridge. After night bathing: night buses 907 and 973.

Plan the Rest of Your Day

Rudas is a 10-minute walk from the Pest side, so it pairs well with a walking tour in the morning or a night cruise on the Danube after your bath. If you hold a Budapest Card, it takes 20% off Rudas day tickets. For more ideas, see the best things to do in Budapest.

Holiday Hours

On 8 December 2026 the bath closes at 16:00. On public holidays it follows weekend hours. The cash desk closes one hour before closing time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is Rudas Baths in 2026?

At the desk, an all-zones ticket is 12,000 HUF Monday to Thursday and 15,000 HUF Friday to Sunday (16,000 HUF on holidays). Fast track is 15,000 / 18,000 HUF. The bath + 3-course meal package starts at €74.99 on weekdays.

Is the Rudas Turkish bath men only?

Only at certain times: men only on Wednesday all day and on Monday, Thursday and Friday until 10:45; women only on Tuesday. It is co-ed on Monday, Thursday and Friday afternoons, all weekend and during night bathing.

When is night bathing at Rudas?

Every Friday and Saturday from 22:00 to 3:00. Tickets are €45–54 on the official site (15,000 HUF on the price list). The saunas are closed at night.

Can children go to Rudas Baths?

No. Rudas does not admit children under 14. For a family visit, choose Palatinus, which has kids' pools.

Is Rudas wheelchair accessible?

No. For technical reasons Rudas currently cannot receive guests with reduced mobility.

Should I book Rudas tickets online or buy at the desk?

The desk is cheapest (12,000 HUF on weekdays). Book online if you want fast-track entry, free cancellation or the meal package, or for night bathing.

Is Rudas better than Széchenyi?

For atmosphere and views, often yes: the Ottoman Turkish bath and the rooftop pool are unique. Széchenyi has bigger outdoor pools and more of them.
