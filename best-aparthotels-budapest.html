7 Best Aparthotels in Budapest (2026, Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
7 aparthotels compared on the kitchen you get, price per night, guest scores and the walk to the centre
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

An aparthotel in Budapest gives you a kitchen and a sofa like a rental flat, with a 24-hour front desk and cleaning like a hotel, and the best ones cost less than a central hotel room. The right one depends on what you need on top: a pool after sightseeing (that is Adina, the #1 pick, €92), the lowest price in the centre, free parking or a two-bedroom flat for a group. This page gets you to the right one in under a minute.

I compared all 7 on what decides an apartment stay: what kitchen the units have, the price for the same night with a kitchen unit, guest scores, parking and how far you are from the sights, all from each property's live listing. Adina is the aparthotel that feels most like a hotel: every studio and apartment has a kitchen or kitchenette, and downstairs there is an indoor pool, a hot tub and a gym looking onto a leafy courtyard. For the same night it cost €92 for a unit with a kitchen, and guests score it 9.3.

The extras suit longer stays: a guest laundry, an underground car park, and a valet service that can stock your fridge with groceries.

Before You Book an Aparthotel

Kitchen or kitchenette: the cheapest unit at Adina, Florin and Marbles may have a kitchenette (hob, fridge, microwave) rather than a full kitchen; the bigger apartments have full kitchens. The room name on the booking page tells you which.

Parking: Florin's on-site parking costs extra and needs an access permit: the hotel asks for a photo of your car's registration at least 72 hours before arrival.

Check-out: Marbles asks you to leave by 10 am, an hour earlier than the others.

Groceries: the Great Market Hall is a short tram ride from all the Pest aparthotels here; Adina's valet service can also stock your fridge.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best aparthotel in Budapest?

Adina Apartment Hotel Budapest is our #1: apartments with a kitchen or kitchenette, an indoor pool and hot tub, and a 9.3 guest score, from €92 a night. Emerald Downtown Luxury Suites has the highest score (9.5) and the best location, at a higher price.

What is the cheapest aparthotel in central Budapest?

Florin Apart Hotel, from €76 a night for 2 adults on a March weeknight, on Károly körút about 500 ft from downtown, with a kitchen or kitchenette in every apartment.

Which Budapest aparthotel has a pool?

Adina Apartment Hotel Budapest has an indoor pool and hot tub. Millennium Court, Marriott Executive Apartments has an indoor plunge pool and sauna.

Which aparthotel in Budapest has free parking?

Silver Crown Hotel & Residence lists free parking. Adina, Escala, Emerald Downtown and Millennium Court have garages, usually at extra cost.

Are aparthotels in Budapest cheaper than hotels?

Often, yes. Four of the seven here cost €76–92 a night for 2 adults with a kitchen, less than most central 4-star hotel rooms on the same date.

Do Budapest aparthotels have daily cleaning?

Florin, Escala and Adina list daily housekeeping, and all seven have a 24-hour front desk.
