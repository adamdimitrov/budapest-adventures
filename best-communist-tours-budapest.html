📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

Memento Park is where Budapest put its communist statues when they came off the streets at the end of the communist era, and without context it can feel like a field of bronze giants. This is the park's own guided tour, and it fixes exactly that: about 70 minutes with a guide from Witness Square through Stalin's Grandstand and its hidden rooms, up to the Waving Balcony and through the Statue Park to the End Wall, decoding what each propaganda figure was meant to tell people.

At €24 including entry, the guide costs you €14.50 over the plain ticket. Per hour it is the cheapest guided option here (about €12). After the tour you can stay to watch the film and see the exhibitions in The Most Cheerful Barrack.

The catch is the journey: the guide meets you at the desk inside the park and the listing puts public transport at about 40 minutes. If that sounds like hassle, the car-transfer tour costs €35 more and handles it for you. The tour runs in rain or shine on gravel paths and is not suitable for children under 10.

For a pair that is about €70 extra to skip roughly 80 minutes of public transport each. If your time in Budapest is short, it is money well spent; if you have a free afternoon and like public transport, it is not.

Getting to Memento Park, What to Bring and What Costs Extra

Where Memento Park is: at the corner of Balatoni út and Szabadkai utca in District XXII, about 16 km (10 miles) from the centre. By public transport it takes about 40 minutes. Only the car-transfer tour includes transport, by car from Batthyány tér and back.

What to bring: Memento Park is outdoors on gravel paths and the guided tour runs in rain or shine, so wear comfortable shoes and weather-appropriate clothing, plus sunscreen in summer and a camera for the Trabant and Lenin photos.

Time needed: the official guided walk takes about 70 minutes, then you can stay for the film and exhibitions in The Most Cheerful Barrack. Add around 40 minutes each way if you go by public transport. City walks run 2–3 hours.

What costs extra: transport and tips on the official Memento Park tour, souvenirs and food on the car-transfer tour. City walks without museum entry (the historian walk and the private tour) pass the House of Terror or Parliament but do not include tickets inside.

Age limits: 10+ for the official Memento Park tour, 14+ for the private city walk. Planning the rest of your days? See our best things to do in Budapest and the Parliament guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get to Memento Park?

Memento Park is on the outskirts of Budapest, about 16 km from the centre at Balatoni út and Szabadkai utca (District XXII). Public transport takes about 40 minutes. The Memento Park tour with car transfer picks you up at Batthyány tér and brings you back.

How much are Memento Park tickets?

A day ticket costs from €10 and includes the park, the film show and photo exhibition in The Most Cheerful Barrack, and the exhibition under Stalin's Boots. The official guided tour with entry is €24, and the guided tour with car transfer is €59.

Is Memento Park worth it without a guide?

Yes if you have read up or just want photos; the €10 ticket includes a documentary and exhibitions for context. The statues make far more sense with a guide, though, and the official guided tour adds one for €14.50 more.

Can I do a guided tour of the House of Terror?

Yes. The Communist History Tour with House of Terror option offers a guided tour inside the House of Terror, or a city walk plus a House of Terror ticket to visit on your own, from €50 with a group of up to 10.

What is the best communist tour in Budapest?

For most visitors the Communist History Tour with House of Terror option: a 2-hour guided walk from Parliament to Liberty Square plus a museum entry for €50. For the statue park, the official Memento Park guided tour (€24) or the car-transfer version (€59).

Is there a private communist tour in Budapest?

Yes. The Communist Budapest Private Walking Tour costs €175 per group of up to 25 with hotel pickup. The historian-led walk also offers private and small-group versions.

Are communist tours in Budapest suitable for kids?

The official Memento Park tour is for ages 10 and up and the private city walk for 14 and up. The Memento Park ticket has no age limit listed, and the original Trabant is a popular photo spot.
