Prosecco Cruise Budapest: The 5 Best Unlimited Prosecco Boats (2026)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Local Budapest Tester
Budapest Local • 7 Cruises Compared Side by Side
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right prosecco cruise is the one that fits your evening: 75 minutes of prosecco, spritzers and beer, an hour of bubbles before a night out, a full dinner with the drinks served to your table, or a calm sightseeing hour without the party. This page gets you to that boat in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I rode 1 of these 7 cruises myself (the #1 pick) and compared all 7 on what decides it: what's unlimited and for how long, food, departure times, docks, age rules and cancellation terms, checked on each seller's own page. Danube Beat is Budapest Boat Party's late-night DJ cruise: about two hours on the river and 2.5 hours of party, with an optional after-party at Morrisons 2.

Ratings differ by site: 4.1 from 1,235 GetYourGuide reviews and 4.9 from 755 Viator + Tripadvisor reviews. Guests consistently praise the DJ and the boat, while the main complaints are bar waits and finding the pier.

What guests say
💬 “I paid extra for the vip/unlimited drinks and it really was worth it.” (Tom, United Kingdom, GetYourGuide, September 2026)
💬 “At first there's a long wait (more than 20 minutes) to order.” (Rafael, Spain, GetYourGuide, June 2026)

How to Get There

  • 📍 Meeting point: Akadémia 2 ponton, Id. Antall József rkp., 1051 Budapest.
  • 🗺️ Tram 2 to Széchenyi István tér (1-minute walk) or Vörösmarty tér (M1), 6 minutes on foot.
  • ⏱️ Last entry is 15 minutes after the start; 18+ with ID, no sandals or flip-flops.
