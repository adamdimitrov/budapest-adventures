By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
9 Jewish heritage tours compared on synagogue entries, Jewish Museum, skip-the-line, length and price

To make the choice easy, I compared all 9 on what really decides it: which doors open (Dohány, Rumbach, Kazinczy, the Jewish Museum), the queue, length, price per hour and group size, checked on each seller's own page. Practical Tips: Opening Hours, Dress Code and What Costs Extra

Opening hours. The official hours given in the #9 listing: from 2 November to 31 December 2026 the Dohány Street Synagogue opens Sunday–Thursday 10:00–16:00 (last admission 15:00) and Friday 10:00–14:00 (last admission 13:00). It is closed on Saturday. Plan any tour with synagogue entry for a weekday and book a morning slot on Fridays.

Dress code. Dohány is a working synagogue. Shoulders and knees must be covered, and men cover their heads; a kippah is provided at the entrance. Covers can be bought on site at Dohány if you arrive in shorts, but it is easier to dress for it.

Bags and ID. Some tours ask for a passport or ID card and do not allow luggage or large bags. Several tours do not allow flash photography.

What costs extra. On the exterior-only walks (#4, #5) and the historian tour (#7), synagogue entry is not in the price; the historian tour lists the Dohány ticket at 14,500 Ft. No tour here includes hotel pick-up as standard.

Access. #1, #3, #4 and #9 are listed as wheelchair-accessible. #2 and #6 are not suitable for wheelchair users.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a tour to visit the Dohány Street Synagogue?

No, but a tour solves the two hard parts: the ticket queue and the context. The priority-entry visit from €40 includes skip-the-line entry, a 45-minute guided visit and the Jewish Museum. For Dohány plus Rumbach and Kazinczy in one go, the 2.5-hour walk with 4 entries from €55 is the most complete.

How much are Dohány Street Synagogue tickets with a guide?

Guided options with entry start at €40 (45-minute guided visit plus the Jewish Museum), €45 (tour with the Jewish Museum and Memorial Park), €49 (private tour) and €55 (four entries including Rumbach and Kazinczy). The official fast-track tour in 8 languages is €65.

Is the Dohány Street Synagogue open on Saturday?

No. In November and December 2026 it opens Sunday–Thursday 10:00–16:00 and Friday 10:00–14:00, and is closed on Saturday. Book tours with synagogue entry for another day.

What should I wear to the synagogue?

Cover your shoulders and knees. Men must cover their heads; a kippah is provided at the entrance. Covers can be bought on site at Dohány.

Is there a Jewish Quarter tour in German or Hebrew?

Yes. The official fast-track Dohány tour runs in English, Spanish, Italian, French, German, Russian, Hebrew and Hungarian. The other tours compared here run in English, with French, Spanish or Russian on some.

Which tour includes the Rumbach and Kazinczy synagogues?

The 2.5-hour walk with 4 entries (from €55) includes entry to both, plus Dohány and the Holocaust Memorial Park. The tour with the Jewish Museum includes Rumbach only on its 4-hour option. Most cheaper walks show both from outside.

Can I combine the Jewish Quarter with the ruin bars?

Yes. The ruin bars, street art and Jewish Quarter tour (from €63, 18+) mixes district history with ruin bar stops, and the €3 Past & Present walk ends inside Szimpla Kert. For a full night out, see our pub crawl comparison.
