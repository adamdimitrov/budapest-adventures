Practical Tips: Getting Up the Hill, Timing and What Costs Extra

  • Where you meet: six of the nine tours start on top of Castle Hill (Holy Trinity Square, Hess András tér or the Savoy Terrace). The night tour (#5), the language tour (#6) and the Castle Bus (#9) start at Clark Ádám tér by the Chain Bridge, on the Buda side at the foot of the hill. No tour here starts on the Pest side.
  • Getting up: walk up from Clark Ádám tér, take the Castle Bus, or join #5, which lets you meet the group at the top.
  • Opening times: Matthias Church closes at 5 pm and often closes during the day for weddings; the Royal Palace museums are closed on Mondays. Book church-entry tours (#1, #4) earlier in the day.
  • Arrive early for St Stephen's Hall: reconstruction in the palace district makes the Savoy Terrace meeting point hard to find, and #3 does not wait for latecomers.
  • What costs extra: tips everywhere, Fisherman's Bastion upper towers on every tour, Matthias Church on all but #1 and #4, food and drinks (except the Dobos torta on #2).
  • What to wear: comfortable shoes for cobblestones and stairs, an umbrella in rain (most walks run rain or shine), warm layers for the night tours.

Finish the day on the river: Castle Hill is at its best lit up from the water on one of the best night cruises in Budapest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Buda Castle tour includes Matthias Church entry?

The Historian-Led Buda Castle Walk (#1, €54) and the Matthias Church and Fisherman's Bastion tour (#4, €39) both include the church ticket and a guided visit inside. On the Castle Hill Tour from the Zero Kilometre Stone (#6) it is a paid upgrade.

Do any tours include the Fisherman's Bastion upper towers?

No. Every tour except the St Stephen's Hall walk stops at Fisherman's Bastion for the view, but none includes the upper towers ticket.

Can I see inside the Royal Palace on a tour?

Yes, on the Buda Castle Walk with St Stephen's Hall (#3, €26). It includes a 30-minute guided visit to St Stephen's Hall, the only fully restored palace interior, and headsets.

How long does a Buda Castle tour take?

Most guided walks take 2 to 2.5 hours. The St Stephen's Hall walk is 1.5 hours, and the Castle Hill Tour with upgrades runs up to 3.5 hours.

Where do Buda Castle tours start?

Most start on top of Castle Hill, at Holy Trinity Square, Hess András tér or the Savoy Terrace. The Vampires night tour, the multilingual Castle Hill Tour and the Castle Bus start at Clark Ádám tér by the Chain Bridge, at the foot of the hill.

Is there a Buda Castle tour for people who cannot walk uphill?

The Official Buda Castle Electric Bus (#9, €18) drives you up and around the hill with 5 stops. The €12 Buda Castle District Walk (#7) is listed as wheelchair accessible, though it starts at the top.

What is the cheapest Buda Castle tour?

The Buda Castle District Walk at €12 for 2 hours (#7). The Royal Cake walk (#2) costs €16 and adds a slice of Dobos torta.
