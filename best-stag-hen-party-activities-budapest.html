9 Best Stag and Hen Party Activities in Budapest (2026, Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
9 stag and hen activities compared on price per person, drinks included, private options and group rules
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right stag or hen activity is the one that fits your group: an afternoon of unlimited beer rolling past the sights (the E-BeerBus, from €29), a night on a party boat that ends in a club, a boat or a pub crawl just for your group, or something that is not drinking at all, like a gun range or a burlesque class. For more choice in one category, see the full comparisons of shooting ranges and pub crawls. This page gets your group to the right booking in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared all 9 on what decides a group booking: price per person at your group size, which drinks are really included, private or shared venue, day or night, and the house rules that catch groups out (costumes, ID, street-drinking fines), checked on each seller's own page. A Stag or Hen Weekend That Works: Day, Evening, Night

Most groups fit one day activity, one evening activity and one night out into each day. Combinations that work without long transfers:

  • Stag, classic: outdoor shooting (#4) in the morning, the E-BeerBus (#1) in the afternoon, then the party cruise with club entry (#2).
  • Hen, classic: burlesque class (#8) in the afternoon, private wine tasting (#7) in the evening, then the ruin bars or the rooftop night (#9).
  • Big mixed group (15–20): the private boat (#3) before dinner, then the private pub crawl (#5).
  • In town on a Saturday: make Sparty (#6) the night out and keep the day light: a thermal bath visit is the hangover cure.

Want something calmer for the morning after? A daytime river cruise or a Segway tour needs no energy.

Budapest Rules That Catch Stag and Hen Groups Out

  • Street drinking and noise are fined. The private pub crawl's listing warns that drinking alcohol on the street, public urination and excessive noise can mean fines of up to €400, and that any fine your group causes is passed on to you.
  • No costumes on the E-BeerBus. Fancy dress, nudity and smoking on board are banned.
  • ID for everyone. The party cruise, shooting range, Sparty and pub crawls all ask for ID, and most activities are 18+.
  • Arriving drunk can cost you the booking. The party cruise can refuse visibly drunk guests without a refund, and the BeerBus and the private pub crawl do not take intoxicated guests.
  • Sparty bars are card-only (their own card). Top up a SpartyPay card first; bank cards are not accepted at the bars.
  • No alcohol before shooting. Book the range before the drinking starts.

Staying together? Big groups often book a party hostel: compare the best hostels in Budapest.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best stag do activity in Budapest?

For one activity, the E-BeerBus (#1): unlimited beer, your own music and the sights for €29 each. For a full weekend, add outdoor shooting by day and the party cruise with club entry by night.

What can a hen party do in Budapest besides drinking?

A private burlesque class (#8, €200 for up to 15), a private wine tasting with charcuterie (#7) or a Saturday at Sparty in the Széchenyi thermal pools (#6).

How much does a stag do in Budapest cost per person?

The activities here run from €18 to €85 per person. A typical day of shooting (€85), the E-BeerBus (€29) and a party cruise ticket (from €23) comes to about €137 before extra drinks and food.

Can we get a boat just for our group?

Yes. The private party boat (#3) takes up to 20 people for €450 an hour with beer, wine and sparkling wine included. For longer private cruises see the private boat tours.

Can stag groups wear costumes?

Not on the E-BeerBus, which bans costumes and nudity. Check each activity's rules before you pack the fancy dress.

Is Sparty good for a stag or hen party?

Yes, if you are in town on a Saturday. Tickets are non-refundable, bars take only the pre-loaded SpartyPay card, and you need swimwear, a towel and flip-flops.
