8 Best Luxury and 5-Star Hotels in Budapest (2026, Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
8 five-star hotels compared on price per night, guest scores, value for money, pools and location
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right 5-star hotel in Budapest is the one that fits your stay: the most included in the room rate (that is Aria, the #1 pick, €263), a full 5-star with a pool for under €200, a room facing the Chain Bridge, or an address on Andrássy Avenue or Váci utca. Travelling on a smaller budget? The boutique hotels start under €100. This page gets you to the right room in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared all 8 on what decides a luxury booking: the price for the same night, the guest score and the value-for-money score behind it, pool and spa, breakfast rating, and how far you walk to the metro, checked on each hotel's own listing. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best luxury hotel in Budapest?

Aria Hotel Budapest has the highest guest score of the 5-star hotels here (9.6) and includes breakfast, its spa and an afternoon wine and cheese. For the classic grand-hotel view, Four Seasons Gresham Palace faces the Chain Bridge.

How much is a 5-star hotel in Budapest per night?

On a March weeknight for 2 adults, from €187 (Kempinski Hotel Corvinus) to €500 (Four Seasons Gresham Palace). Most of the others cost €246 to €334.

Which 5-star hotels in Budapest have a pool?

Aria, Kempinski Corvinus, Four Seasons (infinity-edge), Anantara New York Palace (heated), W Budapest and Al Habtoor Palace list an indoor pool. Párisi Udvar and Matild Palace have a spa or wellness centre without a listed pool.

Which luxury hotel is closest to the Chain Bridge?

Four Seasons Gresham Palace, about 1,650 ft from the bridge on Széchenyi István tér.

Are pets allowed in Budapest's 5-star hotels?

Kempinski Corvinus, Párisi Udvar and W Budapest list pets as welcome; fees may apply.

Is breakfast included at 5-star hotels in Budapest?

Only Aria includes it in every stay. Elsewhere it depends on the rate you choose.
