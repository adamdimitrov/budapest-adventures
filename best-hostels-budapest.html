Custom Pod Bunks & Privacy

Maverick features modern pod-style wooden bunk beds equipped with thick wrap-around privacy curtains, an integrated personal reading lamp, and a dedicated 230V power socket.

Unlike standard squeaky metal bunks, these custom pods feel like your own mini-cabin inside a dorm room, allowing you to sleep undisturbed even in full 8-bed or 10-bed rooms.

Storage Lockers & Luggage

Massive pull-out storage drawers are built directly underneath each bunk bed. They easily fit large 60L–80L backpacker rucksacks or rolling carry-ons.

Bring your own padlock to secure your drawer. Secure electronic luggage storage is also available at reception before check-in and after checkout.

Sleep Quality & Quiet Street

Located on Kinizsi utca in the 9th District, Maverick sits on a calm side street well away from the Grand Boulevard tram noise and late-night party chaos of the 7th District.

Quiet hours are respected after 11:00 PM, making this the single best Budapest hostel for travelers who value a full night of deep sleep.

Room Pricing & Long-Term Rates

Exceptional unit economics: off-season dorm beds start from just €9–€15 per night. Private en-suite double rooms start from €45.

Adam's field test note: I stayed here for 30 consecutive days in March for €280 total (€9.33/night)—unbeatable value for a month-long digital nomad base.

College Dorm Social Atmosphere

The atmosphere feels like an upscale, friendly university dormitory. It is not an intense drinking party hostel, but socializing happens naturally in the common lounge and ground-floor bar.

Ideal for solo travelers who want to meet interesting international backpackers without dealing with 4:00 AM shouting in the dorm corridors.

Digital Nomad & Laptop Workspaces

The ground-floor lobby and mezzanine feature large wooden communal tables with accessible power strips, ergonomic chairs, and stable high-speed Wi-Fi.

Great daytime work environment where digital nomads can take Zoom calls, write, and work comfortably before heading out into the city.

Organized Events & Ruin Bar Nights

The hostel organizes casual evening mixers, game nights, and guided group outings to Budapest's famous ruin bars (Szimpla Kert, Instant-Fogas).

You get the best of both worlds: social nightlife when you want it, and quiet rest when you return.

Guest Kitchen & Cooking Setup

A fully equipped self-catering guest kitchen is available with electric stovetops, microwaves, toasters, water kettles, and cooking utensils.

Operational reality note: The kitchen can get cramped between 7:00 PM and 9:00 PM. Fridges are heavily packed and require diligent food labeling to avoid mix-ups.

Bathrooms & Cleanliness Standards

Modern hotel-grade shared and private en-suite bathrooms. Cleaned and disinfected multiple times daily by dedicated housekeeping staff.

High water pressure and reliable hot water at all hours, with private shower stalls and separate changing spaces.

Rooftop Sun Terrace & Ground Floor Café

A rare bonus in central Budapest: Maverick boasts a spacious top-floor open-air rooftop sun terrace with outdoor seating and skyline views.

The ground floor hosts an on-site café and bar serving espresso, craft beers, and snacks throughout the day.

Theft Warning & Locker Security

Important real-world warning: During my 30-day stay, I had a pullover and a box of nasal strips stolen from an unlocked area. This can happen in any busy hostel.

Always lock your valuables, electronics, and clothing in your under-bed locker with a sturdy padlock whenever leaving the room.

Dorm Layout & Bed Privacy

Hostel One offers spacious 4, 6, and 8-bed mixed and female dorms. Beds feature sturdy wooden construction designed to minimize creaking when roommates climb up.

Beds include privacy curtains and clean linens. Mattresses are thick and comfortable for backpacker standards.

Secure Under-Bed Storage

Large lockable storage cages under each bed accommodate full-size backpacks. Bring your own standard padlock or purchase one at reception.

Note on bedside layout: There are no bedside shelves or wall trays, so keep your phone and charging cable secured.

Nighttime Noise & Curfew Timing

While Hostel One is a party hostel, noise inside the dorms is strictly managed. Pre-drinks and drinking games in the on-site bar wrap up around 11:00 PM–12:00 AM.

At midnight, the entire crowd moves out to the Jewish Quarter ruin bars, leaving the sleeping quarters quiet for anyone resting.

Hostel Categories

Overview — Budapest Hostel Types

Party, Hotel-like, Homely, Social, or Digital Nomad: choosing the right vibe for your Budapest stay

Budapest hostels come in various shapes and sizes, each with its own vibe, amenities, and quality. There's a hostel for everyone's taste, and they usually fall into one of five categories.

Party Hostels

  • ‍ These hostels are buzzing with energy and a love for good times. Alcohol flows freely, but due to district sound-level regulations, they usually quiet down after midnight . The party crowd then typically heads to a ruin bar to keep the fun going. Expect additional activities like pub crawls and boat tours for an extra fee. ‍

Hotel-like Hostels

  • If you're looking for a no-frills , economical stay similar to a hotel, these hostels are a great choice. Ideal for those who prefer privacy over social interaction, they offer a comfortable bed and essential amenities. ‍

Homely Hostels

  • ‍ These smaller hostels , usually managed by one or two staff members, offer a cozy, home-like environment . The atmosphere is more personal and intimate than larger, commercial hostels. ‍

Social Hostels

  • Similar to a college dorm , these hostels are perfect for meeting new people without the constant party atmosphere. They foster a lively, relaxed, and energetic environment encouraging social interaction. ‍

Digital Nomad Hostels

  • ‍ Tailored for the modern traveler, these hostels boast fast Wi-Fi and dedicated workspaces. Recognizable for their minimalist , office-like design , they often feature an in-house bar or café , providing the perfect blend of work and leisure.
Decision Guide

Things to Consider When Choosing A Hostel in Budapest

5 essential factors to evaluate before booking your bed

When selecting the ideal hostel in Budapest, several decision-making factors come into play:

Location

  • All the hostels on the list are centrally located , offering easy access to Budapest's lively city center, nightlife, and infamous ruin bars. Prioritize hostels in quieter side streets away from noisy avenues and tram lines for a good night's sleep.

Price
