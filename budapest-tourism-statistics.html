Budapest Tourism Statistics 2026 (How Many Tourists Visit?)

By Adam Dimitrov · Founder & Local Budapest Guide
Compiled from Official KSH Data • 6 Charts with Data Tables • Methodology
Last updated: Nov 13, 2026

Budapest recorded 7.69 million tourist arrivals and 18.25 million tourism nights in 2025, the highest figures since the current KSH series began in 2021. Arrivals rose 14.2% on 2024, and 86% of guests came from abroad.

This page collects the official Budapest tourism statistics: how many tourists visit, how long they stay, which months are busiest, where visitors come from and how much they spend. Every chart has a data table and a link to its KSH source, so you can check and cite the figures.

How many tourists visit Budapest each year?

Budapest welcomed 7.69 million tourist arrivals in 2025, up 14.2% from 6.73 million in 2024. Arrivals have grown every year since 2021, when pandemic travel restrictions kept the total to 1.72 million.

When do most tourists visit Budapest?

Budapest is busy all year. In 2025 the quietest month, January, still saw 1.07 million tourism nights, while the peak in August reached 1.90 million. December is the second busiest month (1.82 million nights, up 14.5% on December 2024), thanks to the Christmas markets and New Year's Eve. Every month of 2025 was busier than the same month in 2024.

How much do tourists spend in Budapest each year?

Spending is only published for Hungary as a whole. Foreign visitors spent 3,729 billion forints in Hungary in 2025, up 11.6% on 2024 and 61% above 2019 in current prices. Part of that rise reflects Hungary's high inflation in 2022 and 2023, so the real increase is smaller. These figures include day trips across the border, not just overnight stays.

How popular is Budapest as a tourist destination?

  • Budapest ranks 6th on DW's list of the 10 most visited cities in Europe. 
  • Renowned for its stunning architecture, iconic bridges, and historical landmarks, the Hungarian capital continues to attract a large number of travelers.

How important is tourism to Hungary's economy?

Tourism plays a vital role in the Hungarian economy, growing at a faster pace than overall economic growth in the decade preceding the COVID-19 pandemic. 

  • In 2022, the OECD iLibrary revealed that the total gross value added by tourism industries was 6.2%, compared to 6.8% in 2019. 
  • Tourism also remained a significant source of employment, accounting for 383,700 jobs or 8.7% of national employment in 2021, which was a decline from 421,000 jobs in 2019. 
  • Travel exports contributed 18.4% of total service exports, a drop from the 24.2% recorded in 2019.

What are the top destinations to visit in Budapest?

Budapest is Hungary's top tourist destination, with 7.69 million arrivals at the city's tourist accommodation in 2025. Here are some of the most visited places in Budapest:

  • Buda Castle: Known for its Historical Museum and Matthias Church, Buda Castle offers breathtaking views of the Danube River and is a symbol of Hungary's rich heritage.
  • Parliament Building: Famous for its crown jewels and intricate interior, the Hungarian Parliament Building impresses visitors with its stunning neo-Gothic design along the banks of the Danube.
  • Széchenyi Thermal Bath: Featuring medicinal thermal pools and grand architecture, this thermal bath is a must-visit for those wanting to experience Budapest's spa culture.
  • Banks of the Danube: The riverbanks are part of Budapest's UNESCO World Heritage Site, listed in 1987. The Parliament, Buda Castle and the Chain Bridge line both sides, and a one-hour Danube river cruise passes all of them.
  • Fisherman's Bastion: A popular site for its view of the city and statues of Stephen I, Fisherman’s Bastion is known for its fairy-tale-like architecture and scenic vantage points over Budapest.

Sources and methodology

  • Arrivals, nights and length of stay for Budapest come from the Hungarian Central Statistical Office (KSH), which draws on the National Tourism Data Service Center (NTAK) registry. They cover all tourist accommodation: hotels, guesthouses, private rentals and other registered stays.
  • The KSH series for Budapest starts in 2021, so this page does not compare against earlier years.
  • Country-of-origin and spending figures are national (Hungary), because KSH does not publish them for Budapest separately. Spending comes from the Hungarian National Bank's balance of payments data.
  • Figures are rounded to the nearest thousand; percentages are calculated from the published figures.
