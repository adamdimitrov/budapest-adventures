The 6 Best Budapest to Vienna River Cruises (2027)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
6 Cruises • 5 to 8 Days • Christmas & 2027 Sailings Compared
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right Budapest to Vienna cruise is the one that fits your trip: one way from Budapest to Vienna, a cheap Christmas market loop, a summer week on the Danube, or Vienna in a single day. This page gets you to that ship in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared all 6 on what really decides it: direction (only three sail one way), price per day, drinks, tips, transfers and whether you see Melk and the Wachau Valley, checked on each cruise's booking page. Use the quick picks below, or scroll straight to the ranking.

Quick picks

  • Best value: Crucemundo: MS Arena Christmas Markets, from $859, beer and wine included
  • Best one-way: Emerald: Christmas Time on the Danube, tips + transfers included
  • Best Christmas: Avalon: Christmastime Budapest to Vienna, Melk Abbey, from $3,365
  • Best summer: Lueftner: Classical Danube Cruise, $1,767 in August

🏆 #1 Best Value

Christmas Markets on the Danube: 5 Days on the MS Arena, Vienna to Budapest and Back

4.5476 reviews · operator▾

From $859
▾

Pros:

  • Cheapest cruise here by far: from $859 for 5 days, about $172 per day.
  • Drinks by the glass included, not only with meals.
  • Three capitals in 5 days: Vienna, Budapest and Bratislava, all in Christmas market season.

Cons:

  • Tips and transfers extra.
  • Excursion package is free only if at least 10 guests share a language.
  • Starts and ends in Vienna, so Budapest is a stop, not the finish.
Summary & Review Overview ▾

Crucemundo runs the MS Arena on a short loop from Vienna to Budapest and Bratislava and back to Vienna, timed for the Christmas markets. The best price on any departure this season is $859 on 8 December 2026, and even the dearest date ($1,621 on 30 November) is cheaper than every other cruise in this comparison.

Full board comes with draft beer, house wine, soft drinks, water, coffee and tea by the glass, plus a welcome and farewell cocktail and a Captain's Dinner. City tours of Vienna and Budapest and a guided walk through Bratislava are in the excursion package, which is free when at least 10 guests speak the same language. Budget about €7 per person per day for tips. The same ship and route is also sold as a Europamundo package from $969, so booking it as Crucemundo is the cheaper way in. Arriving in Budapest a day early? The city's own Christmas markets run all through December, and our December guide covers the rest.

Summary & Review Overview ▾

Emerald's Christmas Time on the Danube is one of only three cruises that sail one way between Budapest and Vienna. Day 1 is an airport transfer to the ship in Budapest, day 2 a guided coach tour of the city and a dinner with a folklore show, day 3 a walking tour of Bratislava with Slovakian entertainment on board, and day 4 a guided tour of Vienna before the transfer to Vienna Airport.

The fare covers all tips, airport transfers and port taxes, plus wine, beer and soft drinks with lunch and dinner. Direction changes the price more than anything: Budapest to Vienna is $2,985 on 24 November 2026, while the same cruise from Vienna to Budapest is $2,223 on 28 November 2026 and $1,731 on 20 November 2027.

Is a Budapest to Vienna River Cruise Worth It?

If you only want to get from Budapest to Vienna, no: the train is quicker and much cheaper. A cruise is worth it when the trip is the point. For $859 on the MS Arena you get 4 nights on board, full board with beer and wine by the glass and three capitals in Christmas market season, about $172 per person per day, which is less than many central Budapest hotels charge for a room in December.

The one-way cruises cost more but solve the logistics: Emerald includes tips and airport transfers, so you land in Budapest and fly home from Vienna without booking a single taxi. Spending a night in Budapest before you board? A Danube night cruise shows you the Parliament and the Castle lit up from the water (this is the one I'd book). And if the Danube is the start of a longer trip, the Budapest to Amsterdam river cruises sail the same stretch and carry on for another 11 days to the North Sea.

Budapest to Vienna River Cruises: FAQ

Is there a river cruise from Budapest to Vienna?

Yes, but only a few sail one way. Emerald Cruises (5 days) and Avalon Waterways (6 days) run Budapest to Vienna in late November and December for the Christmas markets. The rest of the year, the cruises that link the two cities start in Budapest and continue to Passau (Lueftner), or loop from Vienna or Passau and call at Budapest on the way.

How much does a Danube cruise from Budapest to Vienna cost?

From $859 per person for a 5-day Christmas loop from Vienna on the MS Arena, $1,731 to $2,985 for Emerald's 5-day one-way cruise, $3,365 to $4,114 for Avalon's 6 days, and $1,767 to $3,700 for Lueftner's 8-day cruise from Budapest to Passau, depending on the date.

How long is a river cruise from Budapest to Vienna?

The one-way cruises take 5 or 6 days, with a full day each in Budapest, Bratislava and Vienna. Cruises that add the Danube Bend, Melk Abbey and the Wachau Valley take 8 days.

Which is better, Budapest to Vienna or Vienna to Budapest?

The stops are the same, so choose by price and flights. On Emerald's Christmas cruise, Vienna to Budapest is cheaper: $2,223 on 28 November 2026 against $2,985 for Budapest to Vienna four days earlier.

Are drinks and tips included?

Emerald includes all tips, airport transfers and wine, beer and soft drinks with lunch and dinner. Crucemundo, CroisiEurope and Terranova include drinks on board but not tips. Avalon adds a daily happy hour to drinks with meals, and Lueftner pours wine at dinner.

When is the best time to cruise the Danube between Budapest and Vienna?

Late November and December for the Christmas markets in all three capitals, May to September for long days on deck and green vineyards in the Wachau Valley. In very dry late summers, low water can force a ship change or a coach section, so travel insurance is worth having.
