When a Train or Boat Beats the Tour

Some of these places are an easy public transport ride away. If you don't need a guide, here is when doing it yourself wins:

  • Szentendre: the H5 HÉV suburban train from Batthyány tér takes about 40 minutes and costs a few euros. Do it yourself unless you want the guided walk or the summer boat back.

How to Choose a Day Trip from Budapest

1. Check the season before you book a boat

The boat rides back from the Danube Bend only run in summer: on the budget Danube Bend trip from May to September (Thursday to Sunday), on the lunch version and the Szentendre half day from 1 May to 27 September (Friday to Sunday). Low or high water also cancels them. In winter every Danube Bend tour returns by coach, so compare them on lunch, group size and price instead.

2. Hotel pickup or meeting point

Pickup saves you 20 to 40 minutes of getting to a meeting point before an early start. Five trips include it (Balaton, both two-capital tours, Eger and the Etyek wine tour from central hotels). The Eurama trips offer it as a paid option. Hévíz leaves from Népliget bus station, so allow time to get there.

3. Group size changes the day more than the itinerary

Coach tours keep a fixed timetable. With 7 or 8 guests (forest tour, Balaton, Páty, the ranch) the guide can adjust stops, and you spend less time waiting for the group. Hévíz is capped at 4, Eger is private.

4. Count what is included, not just the price

The cheapest Danube Bend trip is €85 but lunch and the Visegrád Castle ticket are extra; the €109 version includes a 3-course lunch. The ranch (€115) and Etyek (€85) include food and everything on the program, so there is nothing to pay on the day. The two-capitals tours include no meals.

5. Bring ID for anything near a border

The Balaton trip and the Danube Bend lunch tour step into Slovakia, and both Vienna tours cross two borders. Take a passport or EU ID card.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best day trip from Budapest?

For most first-time visitors it is the Danube Bend: Szentendre, Visegrád and Esztergom are under an hour apart, so you see the most for the least driving. The small-group forest tour (from €96, max 7 guests) is the best-rated way to do it; the classic coach tour is the cheapest at €85.

Can you do Vienna as a day trip from Budapest?

Yes. Direct trains from Keleti take about 2 hours 40 minutes, which gives you 7 or 8 hours in Vienna on your own. If you want Bratislava too, a guided tour with hotel pickup covers both in 12 to 13 hours from €177 (more free time) or €189 (guided in both cities).

How do you get to Szentendre from Budapest?

Take the H5 HÉV suburban train from Batthyány tér; it takes about 40 minutes. In summer you can come back by public boat on the Danube. A guided half-day tour costs from €70 and adds a guide and, on summer weekends, the boat back.

Is Lake Balaton worth a day trip from Budapest?

Yes, if you combine it with Tihany. The peninsula, its abbey and the lake views are the highlight, but they are hard to link by public transport in one day. The small-group Balaton and Tihany tour (from €83, hotel pickup) covers Tihany, a lake viewpoint and Balatonfüred; plan on about 10 hours.

Can you swim in Lake Hévíz in winter?

Yes. The lake stays warm all year, about 25 to 36°C, cooler in winter, and the bathhouse and sauna stay open. The Hévíz day trip (from €155) includes the lake ticket and a guide, but expect about 3 hours at the lake out of 8.

Do Danube Bend tours return by boat?

Only in summer. Depending on the tour, the boat runs from May to September on Thursday to Sunday, or from 1 May to 27 September on Friday to Sunday. Outside those days, and when the water level is too high or low, tours return by coach.

Which day trips from Budapest include hotel pickup?

The Balaton and Tihany trip, both Vienna and Bratislava tours and the Eger private tour collect you from your accommodation; the Etyek wine tour picks up at selected central hotels. Several others offer hotel pickup as an option.

What is the cheapest day trip from Budapest?

Among guided trips, the Gödöllő palace tour is cheapest at €60 for 3.5 hours, palace tour included. For a full day, the Danube Bend day trip at €85 is the best value. Cheapest of all is DIY: the HÉV to Szentendre costs a few euros.
