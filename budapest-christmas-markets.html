Budapest Christmas Markets 2026 (Dates, Hours & Prices)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder of Budapest Adventures
Dates and hours from the organisers' websites, checked on 15 Nov 2026
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

Budapest has four Christmas markets worth planning around in 2026: Advent Bazilika in front of St Stephen's Basilica, Vörösmarty Classic Xmas on Vörösmarty tér, the Budavári Advent fair by Matthias Church on Castle Hill, and Advent Óbudán on Óbuda's Fő tér.

Below you get them side by side: official 2026 dates, opening hours for 24, 25, 26 and 31 December, what mulled wine and lángos cost this year, walking times between the markets, and a route that fits three of them into one evening. Each market is followed by the tours and cruises that start next to it, with December dates we checked on the booking calendar.

Staying a few days? Here is what else is on: my picks for things to do in Budapest in December and in November, the best thermal baths for warming up after an evening outdoors, and the New Year's Eve cruises if you are here for the 31st.

Which Budapest Christmas Market Should You Pick?

Only one evening?

Go to Advent Bazilika after 17:30 for the facade projection, then walk 9 minutes to Vörösmarty tér. That covers the two biggest markets. Finish on the river: the unlimited mulled wine cruise leaves 4 minutes from Vörösmarty tér.

In town 24 to 26 December?

Advent Bazilika is your safest bet: open 11:00–15:00 on 24 Dec and 11:00–20:00 on 25 and 26 Dec. Óbuda is closed and Vörösmarty runs short hours.

With kids?

Advent Óbudán: free ice rink, the St Nicholas gingerbread house and weekend family programs. In the centre, the free mini train on Vörösmarty tér.

Shopping for gifts?

Vörösmarty tér has the craft stalls, but only until 28 December. Buy early if you want handmade pieces.

Want the view?

Budavári Advent on Castle Hill. Go in daylight: it closes at 20:00 and the Danube panorama is right next to it.

On a budget?

Order the 1,600 Ft dish each main kitchen at Advent Bazilika has to offer, and the 2026 kids' menu under 1,000 Ft.

A 1-Evening Route: 3 Markets in 6 Hours

For a Friday or Saturday between 27 Nov and 19 Dec, when all three markets are open.

  1. 15:00 Budavári Advent, Castle Hill. Take bus 16 from Deák Ferenc tér. See the stalls and the view before sunset, which is around 15:55 in December.
  2. 16:30 Down to Pest. Bus 16 back to Deák Ferenc tér, then a 5-minute walk to Vörösmarty tér.
  3. 16:45 Vörösmarty tér. Craft stalls and an early dinner: lángos with cheese is 3,200 Ft, goulash in a bread bowl 5,700 Ft.
  4. 18:00 Walk 9 minutes to Advent Bazilika. The facade projection runs every half hour from 17:30; catch the 18:00 or 18:30 show.
  5. 19:30 Mulled wine and the tree. Stay until closing at 23:00 on Friday and Saturday, or swap the last hour for an unlimited mulled wine cruise (from €21) from Dock 11, 10 minutes' walk away.

Total walking in Pest: about 15 minutes. Óbuda does not fit the same evening: give it a weekend afternoon from 10:00, with skates.

Where to Stay for the Christmas Markets

Pick the base by how late you want to stay out: the two big markets are open until 22:00 or 23:00.

🎄 District V: walk to both big markets

Advent Bazilika and Vörösmarty tér are 9 minutes apart, and both sit in District V. Stay here and you walk home after the last mulled wine, no night bus.

Best for: one or two nights, a couple's weekend.

Summary & Review Overview ▾
❓ Do you make the chimney cake dough yourself at the workshop?
💡 No. The dough needs about 75 minutes to rise, so it is ready when you arrive. You roll it, wrap it on the spit, sugar it and bake it.

This 65-minute kürtőskalács workshop is the highest-rated food experience we found in Budapest: 4.9 out of 5 from 1,733 GetYourGuide reviews and 5.0 from 460 on Viator.

You shape and bake your own chimney cake, choose a topping and get coffee or tea and a certificate. It is a good plan B for a cold or rainy market day. Want more than one sweet? Our Budapest food tours comparison puts this workshop next to the market hall and Jewish Quarter tastings tours.

Budapest Christmas Markets: FAQ

When do the Budapest Christmas markets open in 2026?

Advent Bazilika and Vörösmarty Classic Xmas opened on 13 November 2026. Budavári Advent on Castle Hill and Advent Óbudán open on 27 November. Planning the rest of a November trip? See things to do in Budapest in November.

When do the Budapest Christmas markets close?

Advent Óbudán closes on 23 December, Vörösmarty tér on 31 December (craft stalls on 28 December), Advent Bazilika on 1 January 2027 and Budavári Advent on 3 January 2027.

Are the Budapest Christmas markets open on Christmas Day?

Yes, two of them. On 25 December Advent Bazilika is open 11:00–20:00 and Vörösmarty tér 12:00–18:00. Óbuda is closed, and the Castle fair has not published hours for that day.

Which Christmas market in Budapest is the best?

For most visitors Advent Bazilika: the longest hours, a 3D light show on the Basilica every half hour from 17:30, and it is open on every holiday. Vörösmarty tér, 9 minutes away, is better for crafts.

How much is mulled wine at the Budapest Christmas market in 2026?

1,500 Ft at the two central markets, according to the organisers. A plain lángos is 2,400 Ft and each main kitchen at Advent Bazilika keeps one dish at 1,600 Ft.

Are the Budapest Christmas markets free?

Yes. All four markets in this guide are free to enter. The ice rink in Óbuda is free too.

Is there an ice rink at a Budapest Christmas market in 2026?

Yes, at Advent Óbudán on Fő tér: free to use from 27 November to 31 January. The 2026 Advent Bazilika website lists no rink.
