The Five Non-Negotiable Ethics Charter

Our binding editorial standards across every Budapest Adventures review:

01 / Independence

100% Anonymous Booking

We reserve and board under standard consumer names with zero prior notice to boat operators or concierge desks. No VIP treatment, no special seating.

02 / Payment Integrity

100% Out-of-Pocket Payment

We pay retail ticket prices directly out of our own budget. We strictly refuse sponsored press comps, free meal upgrades, and marketing retainers.

03 / Grounded Testing

Seat Sightlines & Freshness Audits

Price-Transparency Anchoring
