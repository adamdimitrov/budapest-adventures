Budapest, Compared Side by Side.
Written by a Local.

Budapest Adventures was founded by Adam Dimitrov to help you pick the right cruise, bath, tour or night out in Budapest. Every guide lines the options up on real prices, start times, meeting points and what is included, and tells you which one to book.

Vacations Don't Have A Return Policy

Why verified, side-by-side guides matter when you visit Budapest:

🛡️

The Missing Safety Net

If you book a disappointing dinner cruise, a crowded bath slot or a pub crawl that skips the bar you came for, you cannot get your evening back. Many travel blogs repeat the operator's description without checking the details.

📸

The Brochure Gap

Operator photos are taken on perfect days. Where I have been myself, the guide shows my own photos of a normal visit and says so, so you know what to expect.

⚖️

Blended Star Ratings

Booking sites put very different products side by side under similar star ratings: an €11 sightseeing loop and a €150 dinner, a €39 bath ticket and a €110 VIP pass. We split them up and compare like with like.

📍 Budapest Resident 💶 Real Prices & Times 📚 28 Budapest Guides

Based in Budapest, Adam Dimitrov founded Budapest Adventures to give travelers candid, practical advice on what to book in the city. He compares every option side by side on real prices, start times and meeting points, and has tried many of them himself, from Danube cruises and thermal baths to walking tours and hostels. Every guide marks which options he tested in person.

His method focuses on the logistics that decide a good day: finding the right meeting point, knowing what a ticket really includes, picking the time slot that costs less, and telling you when the most popular choice is the wrong one for your trip.
