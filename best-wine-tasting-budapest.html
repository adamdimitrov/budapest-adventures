9 Best Wine Tastings in Budapest in 2026 (Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Budapest Local
9 wine and pálinka tastings compared on wines poured, food, price per glass and group size
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right wine tasting is the one that fits your evening: a proper tour of Hungarian grapes in the city, a half-day at a countryside winery, a glass of Tokaji facing the Parliament, or a pálinka flight instead of wine. This page gets you to that table in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared all 9 on what really decides it: what is poured (and whether Tokaji is in it), the food, price per glass and group size, checked on each seller's own page. Filter by city, countryside, Tokaji, under €50 or pálinka below, or scroll straight to the ranking.

City Tasting or Countryside Winery?

City tastings take 1–2 hours, cost €31–60 and can pour wines from all over the country, because a bar stocks bottles from many regions. You get a guided tour of Hungary in a glass, and you can walk to dinner afterwards.

Countryside trips take 3–5 hours and cost €85–96, and you taste only what the villages around Budapest make, but you sit in the cellar with the winemaker. Etyek is about half an hour away and known for its family wineries; Páty, 20–30 minutes out, is a small village of old cellars. Neither trip goes to the Tokaj region, so if Tokaji is the goal, taste it in the city.

Before You Go: Times, Getting There and What Costs Extra

  • Age: the legal drinking age in Hungary is 18, and most of these tastings are not open to under-18s.
  • Fixed slots: Tasting Table runs 6.00–8.00 pm for the 8-wine class and 3.00–5.00 pm for the cheese tasting on most days. Others vary by date.
  • Minimum numbers: Tasting Table, Etyek and Páty need at least 2 guests. If a slot does not fill, you are offered another date or a refund, so solo travellers should book early.
  • Getting there: Smúz is at the Tram 2 Parlament stop, Color Bar is near Gozsdu Courtyard, Páty leaves from Erzsébet tér, and Etyek picks up from 11 central points, mostly hotels. Rókusfalvy needs your own transport.
  • What costs extra: tips, bottles you buy to take home, extra drinks after the tasting, and the optional platter at Color Bar.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best wine tasting in Budapest?

For most visitors it is the Wine the Gap 8-wine tasting: €60 for 8 wines from native grapes such as cserszegi fűszeres, furmint and kadarka, with farm tapas, in 2 hours. Bortodoor is the cheaper 8-wine alternative at €49.

How much does a wine tasting in Budapest cost?

City-centre wine tastings cost €31–60 per person for 3–8 wines, roughly €6–10.40 per glass. Half-day winery trips to Etyek and Páty cost €85–96 including transport and food. A pálinka tasting starts at €20.

Where can I taste Tokaji in Budapest?

Three options promise Tokaj: the Tasting Table evening class ends its 8 wines on a Tokaj aszú, the Tasting Table cheese tasting ends its 5 wines on a sweet Tokaj, and Smúz Café pours 3 Holdvölgy Tokaji wines, including Aszú, facing the Parliament.

Is there a pálinka tasting in Budapest?

Yes. Color Bar runs a 90-minute guided tasting of 3 premium pálinkas (grape 40%, pear 42%, apricot 60%) in 2 cl pours for €20, with olives and water. A cheese and meat platter can be added. Several cooking classes also pour a pálinka on arrival.

Can I visit a winery near Budapest without a car?

Yes. The Etyek trip picks up from 11 central points, mostly hotels and includes 2–3 wineries and a home-cooked meal for €85. The Páty tour drives a group of up to 8 from Erzsébet tér to 1880s village cellars for €96.

Do Budapest wine tastings include food?

Almost all do. The city bars serve tapas, charcuterie or food pairings, the Etyek trip includes a full meal, and Páty serves a farm tapas plate. The exceptions are Color Bar's pálinka tasting (olives only, platter extra) and Rókusfalvy (scones, cheese plate on request).

How long is a wine tasting in Budapest?

City tastings run 1–2 hours. The Páty tour takes about 3 hours including the drive, and the Etyek trip 4–5 hours.
