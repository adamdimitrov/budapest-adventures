2026 Sparty Ticket Guide

Sparty Budapest: Tickets, Prices & Tips (2026)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder & Local Budapest Tester
Budapest Local • Sparty Tickets & 2 Alternatives Compared
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

The right Saturday night in the baths is the one that fits your crowd: Sparty, the pool party in the Széchenyi Baths, Sparty with two drinks in the price, a late-night soak at Rudas, Fridays too, or unlimited beer in the Széchenyi Beer Spa. This page gets you to that ticket in under a minute.

To make the choice easy, I compared all 4 on what really decides it: what you actually get for the money, hours, how you pay at the bar (a SpartyPay card topped up in cash) and refunds (every Sparty ticket is non-refundable), checked on each seller's own page. Scroll straight to the tickets.

Quick Picks: Which Ticket Is Right for You

  • 🎉 You want the party and will drink a few: Sparty Basic, €73, then top up a SpartyPay card at the bar.
  • 🍹 You want one cocktail or two beers: Sparty Premium, €100, coupons included.
  • 🌙 You are free on Friday, or want a calm night soak: Rudas night bathing, 22:00–3:00.
  • 🍺 You want drinks in the water but need a refundable booking: Széchenyi + Beer Spa, daytime.

Sparty at a Glance

  • 📅 When: every Saturday, 21:30–2:00, last entry 1:00. Next dates: 21 and 28 November, 5 and 12 December 2026.

🎉 #1 Best Value Sparty Ticket
Széchenyi Baths, City Park • ⏱️ Sat 21:30–2:00 • M1 Széchenyi fürdő

Sparty Basic Ticket (Entry + Locker)

Rating on GetYourGuide GetYourGuide4.2(1.8k+)→

▾

Pros:

  • Open-air pool party: Techno, lasers, fire shows and circus artists around the outdoor pools of the Széchenyi Baths.
  • Warm water in winter: The outdoor pools are 34–38 °C, so Sparty runs in November and December too.
  • Locker included: A wardrobe-type locker comes with the ticket.
  • Easy to reach: Metro M1 to Széchenyi fürdő, about 5 minutes from the centre.

Cons:

  • Non-refundable: No cancellation or refund on any Sparty ticket.
  • Pricey drinks: Guests report cocktails at about 6,300 HUF, paid only by a SpartyPay card topped up with cash.
  • Deep pool: The pool is about shoulder-deep, so dancing is easiest near the steps.
  • Bring your own kit: Towels and flip-flops are not included.
Summary & Review Overview Overview ▾
❓ Is Sparty worth it?
💡 Yes, if you want one big night out in a thermal bath. Basic is the right ticket if you plan to drink more than a couple of beers.

Sparty turns the Széchenyi Baths into an open-air club every Saturday night: DJs, lasers, fire shows and circus artists around steaming 34–38 °C pools under the yellow palace. It runs from 21:30 to 2:00, and the last entry is at 1:00.

The shows and the setting are the highlight. The two things to plan for are drink prices and the cash-only SpartyPay system.

How to Get There

  • 📍 Állatkerti körút 9-11, 1146 Budapest (Széchenyi Baths, City Park)
  • 🚇 Take metro M1 to Széchenyi fürdő, about 5 minutes from the city centre.
  • 🚕 Going home at 2:00: order a Bolt or an official taxi through an app. Guests report overpriced street taxis waiting at the exit.

Sparty Tips: Before You Go

Pick the Right Ticket

Basic (€73) and Premium (€100) get you into the same party with a locker. Premium adds 2 drink coupons and a cup, which covers 2 beers or 1 cocktail. The official site sells the same tiers a little cheaper (Basic €69, Premium €85) plus Premium Plus (€104, 3 coupons), Express (€135, fast entry and a cabin) and VIP (€199, VIP bar and area).

Bring Cash for the Bar

No bank cards at the bars. Get a SpartyPay card first (first top-up 20,000 HUF, including a 3,000 HUF deposit) and top it up with cash. Drinks come in a reusable cup: 700 HUF, 350 HUF back when you return it.

What to Bring

Swimwear, a towel, flip-flops (mandatory) and a passport or ID card. Food, drinks, large bags and glass are not allowed.

Getting Home

Metro M1 stops running before 2:00, so plan a ride back. Order a Bolt or an official taxi through an app; guests report overpriced street taxis at the exit.

No Refunds

All Sparty tickets are non-refundable. If your Saturday is not fixed yet, Rudas night bathing (Fridays and Saturdays) or the Beer Spa (free cancellation) are safer bookings.

Making a weekend of it?

Stag and hen groups often add a daytime activity before Sparty: compare the shooting range packages by price per round, or see the city from above with a helicopter or gyrocopter flight.

Booking Sparty for a stag or hen group?

Build the rest of the weekend from the stag and hen party activities: unlimited-beer bus tours, party cruises with club entry, a private boat for up to 20 and a private wine tasting.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Sparty in Budapest?

Every Saturday from 21:30 to 2:00 at the Széchenyi Baths, with last entry at 1:00. The next dates are 21 and 28 November and 5 and 12 December 2026.

How much are Sparty tickets?

The Basic ticket with a locker is €73 and the Premium ticket with 2 drink coupons is €100 for Saturday 21 November 2026. The official site sells Basic from €69 and VIP up to €199.

Can you get a refund on Sparty tickets?

No. Sparty tickets are non-refundable. For a refundable option, book the Széchenyi Beer Spa, which has free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

How do you pay for drinks at Sparty?

Only with a SpartyPay card. The first top-up is 20,000 HUF including a 3,000 HUF deposit, and you top up with cash; bank cards are not accepted at the bars.

What is the age limit for Sparty?

18. Bring a passport or ID card.

Is there a night bath party on Fridays?

Sparty is Saturdays only. On Fridays, Rudas has night bathing from 22:00 to 3:00, and Széchenyi is open until 22:00.
